Accessibility tools Basket Skip to main content Bucks Activity Project Aylesbury, Bucks Working with over 350 children in Buckinghamshire, we provide services for disabled children aged 0 - 19. We work with families and children aged 0–19 who have a disability and need additional support. Our mission is simple: to create opportunities for children and young people to enjoy meaningful, fun, and inclusive experiences — while giving families the breathing space they deserve. We offer: Holiday Activities Engaging sessions during school breaks to keep children active, social, and entertained. Weekend Sessions Regular opportunities for children to enjoy structured play and social time. Youth Clubs Safe, supportive spaces for young people to build friendships and independence. Out and Abouts Community-based activities that encourage confidence and exploration. Fun Days Special themed events packed with excitement and creativity. Family Trips Inclusive outings designed for the whole family to enjoy together.
