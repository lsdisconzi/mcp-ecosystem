About US

As the world’s leading private airport operator and subsidiary of VINCI Concessions, VINCI Airports manages more than 70 airports in 14 countries. With its expertise as a global integrator, VINCI Airports develops, finances, builds and operates airports, providing its investment capacity and know-how in optimising operational performance, modernising infrastructure and managing their environmental transition.

"As a trusted, long-term partner, we create lasting value for regions."
Nicolas Notebaert
Chief Executive Officer of Concessions at VINCI & Chairman of VINCI Airports and VINCI Autoroutes

Our
ambition
and
expertises

Mobility plays an essential role in the prosperity, attractiveness and future of territories. Aware of this responsibility, we put our expertise in anticipation, financing and operation at the service of the territories which entrust us with their airports under concession.

Our ambition: to maximize the potential of these infrastructures, in a dynamic of sustainable growth and ecological transition, and to create long-term value for the territories and their inhabitants.

VINCI Airports manages and develops airports: our teams exercise all the field skills necessary to design and operate platforms around the world on a daily basis.
