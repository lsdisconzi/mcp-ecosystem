Reject All Save My Preferences Accept All Powered by Rennes Bretagne Airport Created in 1933, Rennes Bretagne Airport serves destinations all year round with direct scheduled flights to France, Europe and internationally. In 2025, Rennes Airport handled 512,000 passengers. The airport stands out for: The quality of its facilities and expertise, ensuring passengers enjoy a smooth and pleasant experience The variety of destinations it serves, operated by 8 airlines. Its range of services tailored to local businesses, particularly through its freight agency. Rennes Airport has been ISO 14001 certified since 2013, attesting to the efficiency of the airport’s environmental management system. With the aim of decarbonising its activity (already 50% less carbon emissions than in 2016), Rennes airport is gradually replacing its fleet of thermal runway vehicles with electric vehicles, the lighting in the terminal, runway and offices has been replaced with LEDs and 10% of electricity production comes from renewable sources. These initiatives have enabled the airport to achieve Airport Carbon Accredition Level 3 certification in 2022, demonstrating the airport’s efforts to decarbonise its activities and involve its stakeholders in this goal.
