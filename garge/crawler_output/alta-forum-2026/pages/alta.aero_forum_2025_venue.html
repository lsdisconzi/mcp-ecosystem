VENUE

In 1980, Swissôtel was founded by two Swiss companies, Swissair and Nestlé. Since then, it has been growing and expanding throughout the world, and that is how on September 16, 1996 it arrived in Peru and Swissôtel Lima was inaugurated. A few years later, Swissôtel began to be managed by Fairmont Raffles Holding International Inc. and in 2016 it was purchased by AccorHotels, positioning it as a global leader in the hospitality industry and a key player in the luxury hotel market.

Swissôtel Lima is a five-star hotel with a luxurious Swiss touch and classic, elegant accommodation. It is located in the heart of San Isidro, the most attractive cultural, residential and business district of the capital. Our European heritage is reflected through our genuine hospitality, high quality, care and trust that we offer to our guests.

ABOUT LIMA

In Lima everything is in endless movement, and even the past is constantly being rediscovered. Lima offers an extraordinary range of emotions, sensations, colors and flavors: travelers can visit the city’s impressive cathedral, fly over the ocean, enjoy a photogenic sunset, or savor unmatched cuisine.

Lima is a place of converging trends, created by its people and their living culture, where you will find every corner of Peru represented. One visit to Lima can never be enough. Lima, filled with colonial-era riches, is the only capital in South America that faces the sea, and it is hailed as the gastronomic capital of Latin America.
