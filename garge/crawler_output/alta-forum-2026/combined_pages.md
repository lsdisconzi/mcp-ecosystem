## A Tradition of Technical Excellence and Collaboration since 1963.

## An agenda designed to generate real impact

The ALTA CCMA & MRO Conference is the region’s most established meeting for aircraft maintenance and procurement professionals. Since 1963, it has provided a consistent space for airlines and suppliers to connect, exchange knowledge, and strengthen the foundations of aviation in Latin America and the Caribbean.

Each year, the event brings together more than 600 executives, including over 100 airline representatives, to discuss operational challenges, explore practical solutions, and reinforce long-term partnerships.
  
The conference is structured around five core components:

* A focused and up-to-date technical program
* Networking activities that build genuine connections
* Mini-meetings that foster direct business discussions
* Meetings of ALTA’s Maintenance and
  supply chain
  Committees (airlines only)
* A table-top exhibition area for engagement and visibility

## About Mendoza

Mendoza, officially the City of Mendoza, is the capital of Mendoza Province in Argentina. It is located in the north-central part of the province, in a region of foothills and high plains on the eastern side of the Andes Mountains.

Mendoza is the heart of Argentina’s wine country, internationally renowned for its Malbec and other red wines. The region’s many bodegas (wineries) offer visitors tastings, tours, and unique wine experiences.

The city features wide, tree-lined avenues and a mix of modern and Art Deco architecture. Several smaller plazas surround Plaza Independencia, the city’s main square, which is also home to the underground Museo Municipal de Arte Moderno, showcasing modern and contemporary art.
