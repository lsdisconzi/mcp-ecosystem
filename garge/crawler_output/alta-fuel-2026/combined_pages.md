## Official Host

## Agenda designed to generate real impact

The ALTA Fuel & Sustainability Conference is a key annual gathering for leaders and professionals who are driving the energy transformation within the aviation ecosystem.

The agenda fosters dialogue that leads to concrete solutions in fuel supply, environmental sustainability, strategic initiatives, and impactful projects that support the development of aviation in Latin America and the Caribbean.

The 2026 edition will take place at the JW Marriott Hotel in Quito, Ecuador.

[REGISTER](https://alta.aero/fuel/2026/register-categories/)

## Participants

This event is aimed at airline leaders, fuel and service/data providers, regulatory authorities, airports, manufacturers, fuel and sustainability experts. Our goal is to promote strategic dialogue and generate tangible opportunities tailored to the realities and needs of Latin America and the Caribbean.

[AGENDA](https://alta.aero/fuel/2026/schedule/)

## Key topics on the agenda

The 2026 program will address the most relevant challenges for the aviation sector’s energy future in the region. Key topics include:

* Operational efficiency and sustainability
* Energy transition
* Fuel infrastructure and logistics
* Regional and global market perspectives
* Innovation and collaboration

This edition will also foster networking spaces, interactive sessions, and meetings designed to create strategic connections and actionable solutions.

From April 15 to 17, 2026, join us in Quito to keep building a more sustainable, competitive, and decarbonization-aligned aviation sector together.

## ABOUT QUITO

Quito, the capital of Ecuador, is a city rich in history and culture, nestled high in the Andes Mountains. It’s known for its well-preserved colonial center.  Located on the slopes of the Pichincha volcano, Quito boasts a unique blend of historical and natural attractions.  The distinction of being the world’s second-highest capital city, situated at an altitude of approximately 2,850 meters (9,350 feet) above sea level in the Guayllabamba river basin on the eastern slopes of Pichincha.
