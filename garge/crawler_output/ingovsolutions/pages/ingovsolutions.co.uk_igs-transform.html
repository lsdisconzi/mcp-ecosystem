IGS Transform

Driving smarter, faster public service transformation

IGS Transform works with central government departments, agencies and public bodies to deliver complex change - from strategy and digital transformation to organisational reform and performance improvement. Our consultants embed directly into delivery environments, adapt fast, and take ownership where it's needed most. We’re trusted because we deliver visibly, operate with credibility, and stay accountable until the job’s done.

We don’t drop in frameworks or flood in juniors. We deploy experienced consultants who understand public sector delivery under pressure. We work shoulder to shoulder with internal teams, stay focused on what matters, and build capability that lasts - not dependency that lingers.

Our capabilities

Strategy Execution & Planning

We turn ambition into executable plans, from business case through to delivery mobilisation. This means fewer false starts, tighter alignment, and delivery that actually lands.

Digital, Data & Technology Transformation

We support DDaT-enabled change that improves services, unlocks data and modernises operations. Our teams understand both the legacy complexity and the urgency to move.

Organisational Design & Workforce Transformation

We design future-ready operating models, structures and roles that actually work in practice. The result: delivery functions that are clearer, leaner, and built for change.

Change & Transformation Delivery

We lead high-impact change initiatives from inside the work - not from above it. You get senior consultants who embed fast, accelerate progress and stay accountable.

Performance & Delivery Management

We implement oversight and reporting systems that give leaders control and clarity. No more dashboard theatre - just meaningful visibility and intervention where it matters.
