speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Anabel Likay Aeropuertos Argentina Anabel Likay, Operation Process Manager-Airport Coordination / Aeropuertos Argentina Anabel Likay is Operations Process Manager at Aeropuertos Argentina, with over a decade of experience in the airport industry. She is responsible for leading process management, operational excellence, and digital transformation initiatives across a national network of 35 airports. Her work focuses on standardizing and optimizing end-to-end operational processes, integrating Operations, Maintenance, Supply Chain, and Technology teams. Anabel has played a key role in major innovation and transformation projects, including AI-driven and SAP-based solutions aimed at improving efficiency, safety, and operational decision-making. Known for her collaborative and people-centered leadership approach, she is passionate about building diverse teams and driving sustainable organizational transformation through technology and continuous improvement.
