About the event The ALTA Aviation Safety, Flight Ops and Training Summit 2026 will provide the platform for reflection, collaboration, and the implementation of solutions that ensure safe, sustainable, and forward-looking air transport, always keeping humans at the heart of safety and operational excellence. The ALTA Aviation Safety, Flight Ops & Training Summit will be held in the beautiful Mendoza, Argentina, June 16 to 18, 2026. It is located in the north-central part of the province, in a region of foothills and high plains on the eastern side of the Andes Mountains. Mendoza is the heart of Argentina’s wine country, internationally renowned for its Malbec and other red wines. The region’s many bodegas (wineries) offer visitors tastings, tours, and unique wine experiences. The city features wide, tree-lined avenues and a mix of modern and Art Deco architecture. Several smaller plazas surround Plaza Independencia, the city’s main square, which is also home to the underground Museo Municipal de Arte Moderno, showcasing modern and contemporary art.
