speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Enrique Rosende Former LATAM Airlines Group Mr. Enrique Rosende Alba is 72 years old and served as Corporate Vice President of Safety & Security at LATAM Airlines Group until March 31 of this year. He joined LAN in 2009 and played a key leadership role in advancing safety and security practices across the organization. Prior to his tenure in the airline industry, Mr. Rosende had a distinguished 37-year career in the Chilean Air Force. During this period, he held several senior positions, including Head of the Education Division and Director General of Civil Aviation of Chile from 2002 to 2006. Between 2007 and 2008, he served as Vice President of the Latin American Civil Aviation Commission. Mr. Rosende graduated as an Aeronautical Systems Engineer from the Air War College. He also holds academic qualifications in Political Philosophy, Human Resources Administration, Military Sciences, and Administration and Planning of Airport Systems.
