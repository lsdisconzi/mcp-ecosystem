speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Renato Achoa Azul Capt. Renato Achoa, Director of Quality, Safety, and Security / Azul Brazilian Airlines Captain Renato Achoa is the Director of Quality, Safety, and Security at Azul Brazilian Airlines. He leads initiatives to enhance safety and operational excellence. Over the past years, his leadership has been instrumental in reducing incidents through the implementation of advanced safety management systems and the promotion of a proactive safety culture. He has successfully led the IOSA (IATA) Certification process since the company's inception in 2014. He received the SAFETY AWARD of DISTINCTION – “Destaque SIPAER” from CENIPA in 2024, reflecting his dedication to safety. Furthermore, he serves as the President of BCAST (Brazilian Commercial Aviation Safety Team) and was awarded the prestigious Jerome F. Lederer Safety Leadership Medal by the Flight Safety Foundation in 2023. Dedicated to enhancing safety for all, he expands the scope of operational safety with the goal of empowering everyone as agents of quality, safety, and security within the company. Throughout his more than 14 years with Azul, he has held various key positions, including management roles, complementing his extensive experience as a pilot. Renato has built over 35 years of experience in the aviation industry. He is also an Airbus Fleet Captain at Azul, having flown numerous other aircraft types throughout his career. Renato holds an MBA in International Aviation Management from Embry Riddle Aeronautical University and a Law degree. He is a family man with his wife, two sons, and two granddaughters.
