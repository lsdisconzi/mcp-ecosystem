speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Rodrigo Barrios Georgi JetSMART Rodrigo Barrios Georgi, Chief Operating Officer / JetSMART Rodrigo Barrios is Chief Operating Officer at JetSMART Airlines, leading the company’s operations across Chile, Argentina, Peru, and Colombia. In this role, he oversees Flight Operations, Maintenance & Engineering, Airports, Safety, Security & Quality, the Operational Control Center, and Operational Planning. With 26 years of experience in the aviation industry, Rodrigo brings a broad operational and technical background, combining hands-on aircraft maintenance experience with senior leadership roles in airline operations, maintenance, safety governance, and operational performance. Prior to his current role, he served as Maintenance & Engineering Director at JetSMART and held several leadership positions at LATAM Airlines in Chile and Peru. Throughout his career, Rodrigo has led major operational transformation, certification, and reliability initiatives, including the launch of new AOCs, fleet entry-into-service processes, and safety and operational standardization across multiple countries. His experience is strongly focused on building scalable operations, strengthening safety and quality systems, improving operational reliability, and driving efficiency in complex airline environments. He holds master’s degrees in Operations Management and Supply Chain and a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration.
