speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Leandro Oshiro Aeropuertos Argentina Leandro Oshiro, Corporate Operations Manager / Aeropuertos Argentina Leandro is a aviation experienced professional with a strong track record in airport operations and leadership. Over the course of his career, he has held key roles such as National Airports Manager at LAN Argentina, Airport Manager at Ezeiza for LAN and TAM, and CCO Manager at LAN Argentina. He also brings valuable public-sector experience from his tenure as General Director of Airport Infrastructure and Services at ANAC. Leandro holds a degree in Human Resources and an MBA from Universidad de San Andrés.
