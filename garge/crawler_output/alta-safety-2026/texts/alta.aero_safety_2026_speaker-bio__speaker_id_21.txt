speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Andy Hamilton Delta Air Lines Andy Hamilton, Senior Manager of Safety Investigations and Pilot Fatigue for Flight Safety / Delta Air Lines Andy Hamilton is the Senior Manager of Safety Investigations and Pilot Fatigue for Flight Safety at Delta Air Lines. In this role, Andy leads a team of Air Safety Investigators, and pilot fatigue specialists to identify, investigate, and assess operational and organizational risk. Through cross divisional collaboration, Andy assists in the mitigation of hazards and the monitoring of risk controls through safety data. Andy joined Delta Air Lines in 2019, bringing more than 22 years of airline industry experience, with 17 of those years in various safety positions. Andy’s background includes internal safety auditing, regulatory compliance, investigations and was an FAA Part 119 Director of Safety. Andy attended Embry Riddle Aeronautical University, where he received a bachelor’s degree in professional aeronautics with a focus on safety. He also holds a Private Pilot certification and certifications on Accident Investigation from the University of Southern California.
