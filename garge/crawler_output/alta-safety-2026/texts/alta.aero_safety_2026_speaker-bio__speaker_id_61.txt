speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Mariana Olvera Aeromexico Mariana Olvera, Manager, Continuous Improvement, Emergency Response / Aeromexico Mariana Olvera Redding holds a Bachelor's degree in International Relations, a Technical Specialization in Humanitarian Logistics, and master's degrees in Sustainable Humanitarian Action and Business Administration. Her academic and professional background focuses on disaster response, humanitarian action, and crisis management, complemented by specialized training through the United Nations Disaster Assessment and Coordination (UNDAC) program. She has eight years of experience across the public, private, and non-profit sectors, supporting preparedness, response, and recovery efforts in natural disasters, humanitarian crises, and aviation emergencies. Mariana currently serves as Continuous Improvement Manager for Emergency Response at Aeromexico, where she focuses on keeping the company's emergency response processes and plans up to date, ensuring they reflect current risks, operational realities, and industry practices.
