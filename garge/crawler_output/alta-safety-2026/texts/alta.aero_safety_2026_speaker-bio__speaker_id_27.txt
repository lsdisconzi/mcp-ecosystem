speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Raul de Souza ABEAR Raul de Souza, Director of Safety and Flight Ops / ABEAR Raul de Souza holds a Master’s degree in Engineering with a focus on Flight Safety and Continuing Airworthiness from the Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica (ITA), and a Bachelor’s degree in Aeronautical Sciences from the Brazilian Air Force Academy (AFA). With over 35 years of aviation experience, he has worked as an officer in the Brazilian Air Force, flight instructor, transport pilot and senior aircraft accident investigator. He was Flight Data Analysis Manager at LATAM Airlines and acted as Technical Advisor at the Brazilian Airlines Association (ABEAR), where he currently holds the position of Director of Operations and Safety. He has accumulated over 7,000 flight hours, holds international certifications in aircraft accident investigation and has extensive expertise in operational safety.
