speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Francisco Santiago ICAO SAM Francisco Javier Santiago, Licensing Specialist, SAM Regional Office / ICAO Francisco Javier Santiago Pergolesi is an aviation professional with more than 27 years of experience in civil aviation, with a strong background in flight operations, training, certification, operational safety oversight, dangerous goods, and regional cooperation. For the past three years, he has been part of the Regional Safety Oversight Cooperation System (SRVSOP), initially serving as an Operations Specialist and currently as a Personnel Licensing (PEL) Specialist. Throughout his career, he has held positions such as Operations Inspector, Head of Operations Unit, National Continuous Monitoring Coordinator, and focal point for regional and international civil aviation organizations. He has also participated in air operator certification processes, implementation of Safety Management Systems (SMS), risk-based oversight initiatives, preparation for USOAP and IASA activities, and the development of regional initiatives focused on regulatory harmonization, capacity building, and operational safety enhancement.
