Sergio Quito, Safety and Flight Operations Senior Advisor and Safety Review Board Chair / Gol Linhas Aéreas Summary of Qualifications • GOL´S Safety & Flight Operations Senior Advisor and Chairman of the Safety Review Board – SRB since 2020. • VIVA Aerobus independent advisor and Chairman of the Safety Review Board since 2022. • Head of eVTOL implementation project and Joint Working Group with Avolon and Vertical Aerospace since 2021. • Vice President and Chief Operating Officer (COO) from February 2014 until March 2019, responsible for Flight Operations & Flight Attendants, OCC, Airports, Maintenance, Safety, Security and Quality Assurance. • Safety, Security & Quality Director from March 2006 until January 2014, being responsible for Line Operations Safety Audit (LOSA) Program (2006), IOSA certification (2008) and Safety Management Systems implementation (2010). • At GOL Group since 2004, began as Boeing 737-800 NG Line Instructor, still currently as a B737 NG/MAX captain, experience of more than 45 years in Airlines accumulating more than 21.000 hours of flight time, in narrow and wide bodies’ airliners. • IATA Operations Committee member from 2008 up to June 2019, when left as Vice-Chairman. • Chairman of IATA´s Safety & Fight Operations B737 MAX return to service Task Force. • Education: • Portuguese mother language, fluent in English, French and Spanish.
