speaker bio – ALTA Safety Summit BIOS Manuel Muñoz JetSMART Manuel Felipe Muñoz, Corporate Safety Manager / JetSMART Manuel Felipe Muñoz is the Corporate Safety Manager at JetSMART, responsible for leading the company’s Safety strategy across Chile, Argentina, Peru, and Colombia. He is an Aeronautical Engineer with two master’s degrees and has more than 10 years of experience in operational safety management, safety assurance, risk management, event investigation, and the continuous improvement of Safety Management Systems in the aviation industry. Before joining JetSMART, Manuel held safety leadership roles at Avianca Group, where he gained extensive experience in Safety Risk Management, Safety Assurance, operational audits, safety performance indicators, Just Culture, and continuous improvement programs. In his current role, he promotes initiatives focused on predictive analysis, operational risk management, safety performance monitoring, fatigue risk management, flight data analysis, and regional collaboration to strengthen aviation safety in Latin America. His professional focus is on transforming data, operational experience, and industry collaboration into effective decisions that help prevent events, strengthen safety barriers, and promote an organizational culture based on learning, trust, and continuous improvement.
