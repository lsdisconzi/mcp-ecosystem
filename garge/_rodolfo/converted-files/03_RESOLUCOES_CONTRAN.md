# Resolution CONTRAN No. 432, of January 24, 2013

**Provides on the procedures to be adopted by traffic authorities and their agents in the inspection of alcohol consumption or other psychoactive substance that causes dependence, for the application of the provisions of arts. 165, 276, 277 and 306 of Law No. 9.503, of September 23, 1997 \- Brazilian Traffic Code (CTB).**

---

## PREAMBLE

THE NATIONAL TRAFFIC COUNCIL, in the use of the attributions conferred by art. 12, item I, of Law No. 9.503, of September 23, 1997, which establishes the Brazilian Traffic Code, and under the terms of Decree No. 4.711, of May 29, 2003, which deals with the coordination of the National Traffic System.

CONSIDERING the new wording of arts. 165, 276, 277 and 302, of Law No. 9.503, of September 23, 1997, given by Law No. 12.760, of December 20, 2012;

CONSIDERING the study of the Brazilian Association of Traffic Medicine, ABRAMET, about the medical procedures for inspection of alcohol consumption or other psychoactive substance that causes dependence by drivers; and

CONSIDERING the provisions in processes No. 80001.005410/2006-70, 80001.002634/2006-20 and 80000.000042/2013-11;

## RESOLVES.

**Art. 1** Define the procedures to be adopted by traffic authorities and their agents in the inspection of alcohol consumption or other psychoactive substance that causes dependence, for the application of the provisions of arts. 165, 276, 277 and 306 of Law No. 9.503, of September 23, 1997 \- Brazilian Traffic Code (CTB).

**Art. 2** The inspection of consumption, by drivers of motor vehicles, of alcoholic beverages and other psychoactive substances that cause dependence must be a routine operational procedure of traffic agencies.

**Art. 3** The confirmation of the alteration of psychomotor capacity due to the influence of alcohol or other psychoactive substance that causes dependence shall be given by means of at least one of the following procedures to be performed on the driver of a motor vehicle:

**I** \- blood test;

**II** \- examinations performed by specialized laboratories, indicated by the competent traffic agency or entity or by the Judicial Police, in case of consumption of other psychoactive substances that cause dependence;

**III** \- test on a device for measuring alcohol content in alveolar air (breathalyzer);

**IV** \- verification of signs that indicate the alteration of the driver's psychomotor capacity.

**§ 1** In addition to the provisions in the items of this article, witness evidence, image, video or any other means of evidence admitted in law may also be used.

**§ 2** In inspection procedures, priority should be given to the use of the breathalyzer test.

**§ 3** If the driver presents signs of alteration of psychomotor capacity in the form of art. 5 or there is proof of this situation through the breathalyzer test and there is referral of the driver to undergo blood test or clinical examination, it will not be necessary to wait for the results of these examinations for the purposes of administrative ticketing.

## ON THE BREATHALYZER TEST

**Art. 4** The breathalyzer must meet the following requirements:

**I** \- have its model approved by INMETRO;

**II** \- be approved in the initial, eventual, in-service and annual metrological verification carried out by the National Institute of Metrology, Quality and Technology \- INMETRO or by an agency of the Brazilian Network of Legal Metrology and Quality \- RBMLQ;

**Sole paragraph.** From the breathalyzer result (measurement performed), a tolerance margin must be discounted, which will be the maximum admissible error, according to metrological legislation, in accordance with the "Reference Values Table for Breathalyzer" contained in Annex I.

## ON THE SIGNS OF ALTERATION OF PSYCHOMOTOR CAPACITY

**Art. 5** The signs of alteration of psychomotor capacity may be verified by:

**I** \- clinical examination with conclusive report signed by a medical examiner; or

**II** \- verification, by the Traffic Authority agent, of the signs of alteration of psychomotor capacity under the terms of Annex II.

**§ 1** To confirm the alteration of psychomotor capacity by the Traffic Authority agent, not only one sign should be considered, but a set of signs that prove the driver's condition.

**§ 2** The signs of alteration of psychomotor capacity mentioned in item II must be described in the traffic ticket or in a specific term containing the minimum information indicated in Annex II, which must accompany the traffic ticket.

**Art. 6** The infraction provided for in art. 165 of the CTB shall be characterized by:

**I** \- blood test showing any concentration of alcohol per liter of blood;

**II** \- breathalyzer test with measurement performed equal to or greater than 0.05 milligrams of alcohol per liter of exhaled alveolar air (0.05 mg/L), discounted the maximum admissible error under the terms of the "Reference Values Table for Breathalyzer" contained in Annex I;

**III** \- signs of alteration of psychomotor capacity obtained in the form of art. 5\.

**Sole paragraph.** The penalties and administrative measures provided for in art. 165 of the CTB shall be applied to the driver who refuses to submit to any of the procedures provided for in art. 3, without prejudice to the incidence of the crime provided for in art. 306 of the CTB if the driver presents signs of alteration of psychomotor capacity.

## ON THE CRIME

**Art. 7** The crime provided for in art. 306 of the CTB shall be characterized by any of the procedures below:

**I** \- blood test showing result equal to or greater than 6 (six) decigrams of alcohol per liter of blood (6 dg/L);

**II** \- breathalyzer test with measurement performed equal to or greater than 0.34 milligrams of alcohol per liter of exhaled alveolar air (0.34 mg/L), discounted the maximum admissible error under the terms of the "Reference Values Table for Breathalyzer" contained in Annex I;

**III** \- examinations performed by specialized laboratories, indicated by the competent traffic agency or entity or by the Judicial Police, in case of consumption of other psychoactive substances that cause dependence;

**IV** \- signs of alteration of psychomotor capacity obtained in the form of art. 5\.

**§ 1** The occurrence of the crime mentioned in the *caput* does not preclude the application of the provisions of art. 165 of the CTB.

**§ 2** Once the crime mentioned in this article is established, the driver and witnesses, if any, shall be forwarded to the Judicial Police, accompanied by the evidentiary elements.

## ON THE TRAFFIC TICKET

**Art. 8** In addition to the requirements established in specific regulations, the traffic ticket issued as a result of the infraction provided for in art. 165 of the CTB must contain:

**I** \- in case of referral of the driver for blood test, clinical examination or specialized laboratory examination, the reference to this procedure;

**II** \- in the case of art. 5, the signs of alteration of psychomotor capacity mentioned in Annex II or the reference to the filling of the specific term mentioned in § 2 of art. 5;

**III** \- in the case of the breathalyzer test, the make, model and serial number of the device, test number, measurement performed, the value considered and the regulated limit in mg/L;

**IV** \- as the case may be, the identification of the witness(es), if there were photos, videos or other complementary means of evidence, if there was refusal by the driver, among other available information.

**§ 1** The documents generated and the result of the examinations mentioned in item I must be attached to the traffic ticket.

**§ 2** In the case of the breathalyzer test, to fill in the "Value Considered" field of the traffic ticket, the admissible error margins must be observed, under the terms of the "Reference Values Table for Breathalyzer" contained in Annex I.

## ON ADMINISTRATIVE MEASURES

**Art. 9** The vehicle shall be retained until the presentation of a qualified driver, who will also be subjected to inspection.

**Sole paragraph.** If a qualified driver does not appear or the agent verifies that he is not fit to drive, the vehicle shall be collected to the depository of the agency or entity responsible for the inspection, upon receipt.

**Art. 10\.** The driving document shall be collected by the agent, upon receipt, and shall remain under custody of the traffic agency or entity responsible for the ticketing until the driver proves that he does not have altered psychomotor capacity, under the terms of this Resolution.

**§ 1** If the driver does not appear before the traffic agency or entity responsible for the ticketing within 5 (five) days from the date of the infraction, the document shall be forwarded to the executive traffic agency responsible for its registration, where the driver must seek his document.

**§ 2** The information mentioned in § 1 must be included in the receipt for collection of the driving document.

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Art. 11\.** It is mandatory to perform the blood alcohol test for fatal victims of traffic accidents.

**Art. 12\.** The acts performed during the validity of CONTRAN Deliberation No. 133, of December 21, 2012, are convalidated, with recognition of the tolerance margin mentioned in art. 1 of the CONTRAN Deliberation referred to in the *caput* (0.10 mg/L) as the regulated limit.

**Art. 13\.** The following are revoked: CONTRAN Resolutions No. 109, of November 21, 1999, and No. 206, of October 20, 2006, and CONTRAN Deliberation No. 133, of December 21, 2012\.

# Resolution CONTRAN No. 900, of March 9, 2022

**Consolidates the norms on the standardization of procedures for the presentation of prior defense and appeal, in 1st and 2nd instances, against the imposition of written warning penalties and traffic fines**

---

## OFFICIAL GAZETTE OF THE UNION

**Published on: 03/17/2022 | Edition: 52 | Section: 1 | Page: 46** **Agency: Ministry of Infrastructure/National Traffic Council**

---

THE NATIONAL TRAFFIC COUNCIL (CONTRAN), using the competence conferred by item I of art. 12 of Law No. 9.503, of September 23, 1997, which establishes the Brazilian Traffic Code (CTB), and based on what appears in the administrative process No. 50000.034188/2021-09, resolves:

**Art. 1** This Resolution consolidates the norms on the standardization of procedures for the presentation of prior defense and appeal, in 1st and 2nd instances, against the imposition of written warning penalties and traffic fines.

**Art. 2** The following are legitimate parties to present prior defense or appeal in 1st and 2nd instances against the imposition of a written warning penalty or fine:

**I** \- the natural or legal person owner of the vehicle;

**II** \- the driver, duly identified;

**III** \- the shipper, when exclusively or jointly responsible for the infraction; and

**IV** \- the carrier, when exclusively or jointly responsible for the infraction.

**§ 1** For the purposes of §§ 4 and 6 of art. 257 of the CTB, the shipper is considered the sender or dispatcher of the cargo, even if the freight is to be paid.

**§ 2** The legitimate party mentioned in the *caput* may be represented by a legally qualified attorney or by a power of attorney, in accordance with the law, under penalty of non-acceptance of the prior defense or appeal.

**Art. 3** The request for prior defense or appeal must be presented in writing, legibly, within the established deadline, containing at least the following data:

**I** \- name of the traffic agency or entity responsible for the ticketing or for the application of the written warning penalty or fine;

**II** \- name, full address with ZIP code, telephone number, identification document number and CPF or CNPJ of the applicant;

**III** \- vehicle license plate and Traffic Infraction Ticket (AIT) number;

**IV** \- exposition of the facts, legal grounds and/or documents that prove the allegation;

**V** \- date of the request; and

**VI** \- signature of the applicant or his legal representative.

**Sole paragraph.** The request for prior defense or appeal must have only one AIT as its object.

**Art. 4** The prior defense or appeal will not be accepted when:

**I** \- they are presented outside the legal deadline;

**II** \- legitimacy is not proven;

**III** \- there is no signature of the appellant or his legal representative; and

**IV** \- there is no request, or it is incompatible with the factual situation.

**Art. 5** The prior defense or appeal must be presented with the following documents:

**I** \- request for prior defense or appeal;

**II** \- copy of the ticketing notification or penalty notification, as the case may be, or copy of the AIT or document containing the vehicle license plate and the AIT number;

**III** \- copy of the CNH or other identification document that proves the applicant's signature;

**IV** \- document proving representation, when a legal entity; and

**V** \- power of attorney, when applicable.

**Sole paragraph.** In the presentation of defense or appeal, at any stage of the process, for purposes of admissibility, documents or copies of documents issued by the agency responsible for the ticketing will not be required.

**Art. 6** The prior defense or appeal must be filed with the ticketing traffic agency or entity or sent by mail to its address, respecting the provisions of art. 287 of the CTB.

**§ 1** For verification of timeliness, the following shall be considered:

**I** \- the date of delivery to the Brazilian Post and Telegraph Company (ECT), in the case of prior defense or appeal presented by mail; or

**II** \- the date of filing with the traffic agency or entity of the owner's or offender's residence or domicile, when using the form provided for in art. 287 of the CTB.

**§ 2** For the purposes of item II of § 1, the receipt protocol of the prior defense or appeal must contain at least the identification and signature of the receiver, the identification of the traffic agency or entity and the date of receipt.

**§ 3** The prior defense or appeal received in the form of item II of § 1 must be immediately sent to the agency or entity that issued the ticketing.

**§ 4** The filing of prior defense or appeal may be done electronically, provided it is made available by the traffic agency or entity that issued the ticketing.

**Art. 7** The processes of prior defense and appeal, after being judged and together with the result of their consideration, must remain with the ticketing agency or with its Administrative Board of Infraction Appeals (JARI).

**Art. 8** The prior defense or appeal regarding a vehicle registered with another executive traffic agency must remain filed with the ticketing traffic agency or entity or its JARI.

**Art. 9** The traffic agency or entity and the appellate bodies may request the applicant to present documents or other evidence admitted in law, establishing a deadline for their presentation.

**Sole paragraph.** If the request mentioned in the *caput* is not complied with, the prior defense or appeal will be analyzed and judged in its current state.

**Art. 10\.** The traffic agency or entity or the appellate bodies must supply any missing information or document, when available.

**Art. 11\.** The applicant may withdraw, in writing, until the judgment is held, the prior defense or appeal presented.

**Art. 12\.** The presentation of prior defense or appeal through the Electronic Notification System (SNE), referred to in arts. 282-A and 284 of the CTB, must comply with specific regulations established by CONTRAN.

**Art. 13\.** The following CONTRAN Resolutions are revoked:

**I** \- No. 299, of December 4, 2008; and

**II** \- No. 692, of September 27, 2017\.

**Art. 14\.** This Resolution comes into force on April 1, 2022\.

# Resolution CONTRAN No. 911, of March 28, 2022

**Provides on the permission for the traffic of new vehicles, domestic or imported, before registration and licensing, on the traffic of used incomplete vehicles, domestic or imported, before transfer and on the remount of new vehicles.**

---

## OFFICIAL GAZETTE OF THE UNION

**Published on: 04/01/2022 | Edition: 63 | Section: 1 | Page: 92** **Agency: Ministry of Infrastructure/National Traffic Council**

---

THE NATIONAL TRAFFIC COUNCIL (CONTRAN), in the use of the competence conferred by item I of art. 12 of Law No. 9.503, of September 23, 1997, which establishes the Brazilian Traffic Code (CTB), based on what appears in the administrative process No. 50000.033780/2021-85, resolves:

## CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Art. 1** This Resolution provides on the permission for the traffic of new vehicles, domestic or imported, before registration and licensing, on the traffic of used incomplete vehicles, domestic or imported, before transfer and on the remount of new vehicles.

**Art. 2** The following is permitted, under the terms of this Resolution:

**I** \- the traffic of new vehicles, domestic or imported, before registration and licensing;

**II** \- the traffic of used incomplete vehicles, domestic or imported, before transfer; and

**III** \- the remount of new vehicles.

**§ 1** The permission mentioned in the *caput* extends to new unfinished or used incomplete vehicles, during the daytime, on the route between the following destinations:

**I** \- manufacturer's yard;

**II** \- dealer;

**III** \- reseller;

**IV** \- bodybuilder;

**V** \- final completer;

**VI** \- customs post;

**VII** \- final customer; or

**VIII** \- location for transport to one of the mentioned recipients.

**Art. 3** For the purposes of this Resolution, the following definitions shall be adopted:

**I** \- new vehicle: motor vehicle, electric vehicle, trailer and semi-trailer, before its registration and licensing;

**II** \- finished vehicle: motor vehicle, electric vehicle, trailer and semi-trailer, which leaves the factory ready for registration and licensing, and does not need complementation;

**III** \- unfinished vehicle: chassis and platform for buses or minibuses or chassis of trucks, pickup trucks, utility vehicles, with complete cab, incomplete cab or without cab, which need complementation before registration and licensing; and

**IV** \- used incomplete vehicle: motor vehicle, electric vehicle, trailer and semi-trailer, already registered and licensed, which is in a physical condition analogous to an unfinished vehicle and needs complementation for the effective transfer of ownership.

## CHAPTER II

## ON THE TRAFFIC OF NEW VEHICLES

**Art. 4** New vehicles, including unfinished ones, domestic or imported, before registration and licensing, destined for the final customer, to travel on public roads must mandatorily carry the Electronic Invoice Auxiliary Document (DANFe) or the customs document, as the case may be.

**§ 1** The traffic will be authorized only from the origin to the traffic agency of the destination Municipality where the vehicle will be registered, and may travel for 15 (fifteen) consecutive days, or in the case of the States of the Northern Region of the Country, for 30 (thirty) consecutive days.

**§ 2** The deadline shall be counted from the date of departure of the vehicle recorded in its own field or by means of a stamp on the DANFe or the customs document by the manufacturer's yard, dealer, reseller, bodybuilder, final completer or customs post.

**§ 3** In the case of a new vehicle purchased directly by the buyer through electronic means, the deadline shall be counted from the date of effective delivery of the vehicle to the owner, defined by means of registration in its own field or by a stamp on the fiscal purchase document.

**§ 4** In the case of a new vehicle donated by governmental agencies or entities, the destination Municipality for registration with the traffic agency shall be the one stated in the donation instrument, a copy of which must accompany the vehicle during the journey.

**§ 5** Bodybuilding industries are equated to companies responsible for installing equipment intended for the conversion of emergency vehicles.

**§ 6** Vehicles consigned to dealers for commercialization, and vehicles acquired by private and public entities, to be licensed in the private and official categories, may only carry their own cargo and people who have an employment relationship with them.

**§ 7** In the absence of a departure date on the fiscal document, its issuance date shall be considered.

**Art. 5** The traffic of new vehicles, including unfinished ones, domestic or imported, before registration and licensing, is authorized from the manufacturer's yard, dealer, reseller, bodybuilder, final completer or customs post to the location where it will be loaded as cargo, by any means of transport, and from the unloading location to dealers, bodybuilding industries or final completer, according to the deadline in § 1 of article 4, proven by carrying the DANFe or the customs document.

## CHAPTER III

## ON REMUNERATED TRANSPORT OF CARGO AND PASSENGERS

**Art. 6** The traffic of new finished vehicles, domestic or imported, before the first registration and licensing, transporting passengers or cargo, in a remunerated manner, is permitted provided that the following conditions are observed:

**I** \- the final buyer obtains from the executive traffic agencies and entities of the States and the Federal District of origin of the vehicle, the Vehicle Traffic Authorization (ATV), in the form of Annex I, based on the fiscal document, valid for 15 (fifteen) consecutive days from the date of issuance, which may be extended for an equal period, by the same agency, for reasons of force majeure;

**II** \- the ATV will be issued in 2 (two) copies, of which the first must be carried in the vehicle and the second filed with the issuing agency, and may be a physical or electronic document; and

**III** \- the ATV will only authorize traffic on the route between the Municipality of acquisition and the destination Municipality where the vehicle must be registered.

**§ 1** Vehicles acquired by self-employed individuals and by companies that provide cargo and passenger transport may carry out remunerated services for which they are authorized, subject to specific legislation, the requirements of the granting authorities and the authorities with jurisdiction over public roads.

**§ 2** Remunerated transport of cargo and passengers in new unfinished vehicles is prohibited.

## CHAPTER IV

## ON THE TRAFFIC OF VEHICLES BETWEEN ESTABLISHMENTS OF INTERLINKED LEGAL ENTITIES

**Art. 7** The traffic of new vehicles, including unfinished ones, domestic or imported, before the first registration, from one establishment to another of the same manufacturer, bodybuilder, dealer or interlinked legal entity, requiring the carrying of the DANFe or the customs document, as the case may be.

**§ 1** The vehicle may travel for 15 (fifteen) consecutive days, or in the case of the States of the Northern Region of the Country for 30 (thirty) consecutive days, counted from the date of departure of the vehicle recorded in its own field or by means of a stamp on the DANFe or the customs document.

**§ 2** For newly produced vehicles, benefiting from special tax regimes and for which the sales invoices have not yet been issued, traffic is permitted only from the internal yards of manufacturers and fabricators to the external yards of manufacturers and fabricators or of the companies responsible for transporting the vehicles, within a maximum radius of 10 (ten) kilometers, unaccompanied by a fiscal document, provided they are accompanied by the production list containing the chassis number.

## CHAPTER V

## ON THE TRANSPORT OF A NEW MOTOR VEHICLE BY ANOTHER SIMILAR VEHICLE (REMOUNT)

**Art. 8** The transport of a new unfinished motor vehicle by another new unfinished vehicle, with or without a cab, may be carried out provided it complies with the conditions established in this Resolution.

**§ 1** The set formed by the transporting vehicle and the transported vehicle may not exceed 2.60 meters in width, 4.40 meters in height and 14.00 meters in length.

**§ 2** The rear longitudinal overhang, measured between the vertical plane passing through the original rear part of the transported vehicle and the rear limit of the transporting vehicle, must be at most 3.00 meters.

**§ 3** The assembly service (transported vehicle on the transporting vehicle) must be executed in accordance with the technical recommendations of the vehicle manufacturers, in compliance with the project of a mechanical engineer who will be responsible, together with the transport company, for the stability and operational safety conditions of the set.

**§ 4** The transporting vehicle must have all mandatory equipment established in Annex II, including a rear bumper specially designed for this type of set, installed on the chassis of the transported vehicle and anchored on the chassis of the transporting vehicle, complying with the provisions of the CONTRAN Resolution regarding bumpers.

**§ 5** In new unfinished vehicles, when they do not have a cab or it is incomplete, the driver must wear a motorcycle helmet, with visor or with the use of protective glasses, as established in the CONTRAN Resolution regarding motorcycle helmets, and traffic on rainy days, with fog or heavy mist, as well as nighttime traffic, is prohibited.

## CHAPTER VI

## TRAFFIC OF NEW VEHICLES BETWEEN BORDERS

**Art. 9** The circulation of trucks, tractor-trucks, buses and minibuses, bus platforms, bus chassis, minibus chassis and truck chassis, trailers and semi-trailers, new, destined for export, between the manufacturer, transformer or bodybuilder and the national border or loading location is authorized.

**§ 1** The circulation of these vehicles must be preceded by communication to the executive and road traffic agencies and entities with jurisdiction over the stretches of the itinerary to be covered in national territory, with a minimum advance of five business days.

**§ 2** For the circulation of new vehicles destined for export, the executive and road traffic agencies and entities, within the scope of their respective jurisdiction, may determine safety measures for their circulation.

**Art. 10\.** Proof that the vehicle is destined for export, identified by its chassis number and/or bodywork, shall be given by presenting the DANFe or invoice issued by the manufacturer, and it is mandatory to carry one of these documents, as the case may be.

## CHAPTER VII

## ON INFRACTIONS TO THE CTB

**Art. 11\.** Non-compliance with the provisions of this Resolution will imply, as the case may be, the application to the offender of the penalties and administrative measures provided for in the Brazilian Traffic Code (CTB):

**I** \- art. 169: when traveling, under the conditions established in § 5 of art. 8, without wearing a helmet, with absence or poor condition of the helmet visor or protective glasses, improperly using the helmet or using a helmet that is not a motorcycle helmet;

**II** \- art. 187, I: when traveling with an unfinished vehicle or remount at night;

**III** \- art. 230, V: when traveling with the vehicle out of the registration deadline, without the issuance of the fiscal document, not in accordance with the purpose, or outside the itinerary;

**IV** \- art. 230, IX: when the lack, inoperability or inefficiency of any mandatory equipment provided for in the CTB or in CONTRAN Resolutions is verified;

**V** \- art. 230, X: when non-compliance of any of the mandatory equipment provided for in the CTB or in CONTRAN Resolutions is verified; and

**VI** \- art. 232: when failing to carry any of the mandatory documents provided for in this Resolution.

**Sole paragraph.** The infraction situations described in the items of this article do not exclude the possibility of applying other penalties provided for in the CTB.

## CHAPTER VIII

## FINAL PROVISIONS

**Art. 12\.** Traffic, before registration and licensing, of a new vehicle, domestic or imported, for any purpose other than those provided for in this Resolution is prohibited.

**Art. 13\.** On the routes between the chassis/platform manufacturer, assembler, bodybuilder or final implementer and the destination Municipality, traffic on public roads without spare tire and rim, jack and wheel wrench equipment is permitted for the following vehicles:

**I** \- buses and minibuses;

**II** \- trucks with specific characteristics for the transport of garbage and concrete;

**III** \- armored body vehicles for the transport of valuables; and

**IV** \- vehicles equipped with tires capable of traveling without air, or with an emergency inflation device.

**Art. 14\.** New unfinished vehicles that do not have the minimum mandatory equipment required in Annex II and do not comply with the provisions of this Resolution must be transported as cargo.

**Art. 15\.** The Annexes to this Resolution are available on the electronic website of the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union.

**Art. 3** Semi-trailers towed by motorcycles and scooters must have the following characteristics:

**I** \- identification elements:

**a)** Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) engraved on the semi-trailer structure;

**b)** vehicle manufacturing year engraved in four digits; and

**II** \- mandatory equipment:

**a)** rear bumper;

**f)** rear plate illumination;

**g)** rear direction indicator lamps, amber or red in color;

**h)** tires that offer safety conditions; and

**i)** retroreflective elements applied to the sides and rear of the vehicle, according to requirements contained in a specific CONTRAN Resolution; and

**III** \- maximum dimensions:

**a)** maximum width: 1.15 m;

**b)** maximum height: 0.90 m; and

**c)** maximum total length, including the coupling drawbar: 2.15 m.

**Art. 4** It is up to the traffic authority to decide on the circulation of motorcycles and scooters with a semi-trailer coupled on the road under its jurisdiction.

**Art. 5** Non-compliance with the provisions of this Resolution will imply, as the case may be, the application to the offender of the penalties and administrative measures provided for in the Brazilian Traffic Code \- CTB:

**I** \- art. 230, IX: semi-trailer without any of the mandatory equipment established in this Resolution;

**II** \- art. 230, X: semi-trailer with any of the mandatory equipment in disagreement with the specifications established in this Resolution; and

**III** \- art. 244, VI: motorcycle or scooter towing a semi-trailer in disagreement with the provisions of art. 2\.

**Sole paragraph.** The infraction situations described in the *caput* do not exclude the possibility of applying other penalties provided for in the CTB.

**Art. 6** The following CONTRAN Resolutions are revoked:

**I** \- No. 69, of September 25, 1998;

# Resolution CONTRAN No. 918, of March 28, 2022

**Consolidates the norms on procedures for the application of fines for infractions, the collection and transfer of collected amounts, under the terms of the Brazilian Traffic Code (CTB).**

---

## OFFICIAL GAZETTE OF THE UNION

**Published on: 04/01/2022 | Edition: 63 | Section: 1 | Page: 98** **Agency: Ministry of Infrastructure/National Traffic Council**

---

THE NATIONAL TRAFFIC COUNCIL (CONTRAN), in the use of the competence conferred by items I, II and VIII of art. 12, of Law No. 9.503, of September 23, 1997, which establishes the Brazilian Traffic Code (CTB), based on what appears in the administrative process No. 50000.031757/2021-56, resolves:

## CHAPTER I

## PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Art. 1** This Resolution consolidates the norms on procedures for the application of fines for infractions, the collection and transfer of collected amounts, under the terms of item VIII of art. 12 of the Brazilian Traffic Code (CTB).

**Art. 2** For the purposes provided for in this Resolution, the following is understood as:

**I** \- Traffic Infraction Ticket (AIT): document that initiates the administrative process for the imposition of punishment, as a result of any infraction to traffic legislation;

**II** \- Ticketing Notification (NA): procedure that gives notice to the vehicle owner that a traffic infraction has been committed with his vehicle;

**III** \- Penalty Notification (NP): procedure that gives notice of the imposition of penalty, as well as indicating the amount of the traffic fine;

**IV** \- ticketing agency: agency or entity competent to ticket the owner or driver for committing a traffic infraction, judge the defense of the ticketing and apply the traffic fine penalties; and

**V** \- collecting agency: agency or entity that carries out the collection and receipt of the traffic fine, of its competence or of third parties, being responsible for the transfer of 5% (five percent) of the value of the traffic fine to the account of the National Traffic Safety and Education Fund (FUNSET), as provided in § 1 of art. 320 of the CTB.

**Art. 3** Once the infraction is verified by the traffic authority or its agent, or its occurrence is proven by electronic device or audiovisual equipment, chemical reactions or any other available technological means, previously regulated by CONTRAN, the AIT shall be issued, which must contain the minimum data defined by art. 280 of the CTB and in specific regulations.

**§ 1** The AIT mentioned in the *caput* may be issued by the traffic authority or its agent:

**I** \- by annotation in its own document;

**II** \- by registration in an electronic booklet isolated or coupled to an infraction detection device regulated by CONTRAN, complying with the procedure defined by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union; or

**III** \- by registration in an electronic data processing system, when the infraction is proven by a detection device provided with an image recorder, regulated by CONTRAN.

**§ 2** The ticketing agency, whenever possible, must print the AIT issued in the forms provided for in items II and III of § 1 to initiate the administrative process provided for in Chapter XVIII of the CTB, and the signature of the authority or its agent is waived.

**§ 3** The registration of the infraction, referred to in item III of § 1, will be endorsed by a traffic authority, or its agent, who will be identified in the AIT.

**§ 4** Whenever possible, the driver will be identified at the time of issuing the AIT.

**§ 5** The AIT will be valid as an NA when signed by the driver and he is the owner of the vehicle or the previously identified principal driver, provided that the date of the deadline for the presentation of the defense of the ticketing is indicated, under the terms of art. 281-A of the CTB.

**§ 6** The electronic booklet provided for in item II of § 1 consists of a computerized system (software) installed in equipment prepared for this purpose or in the infraction recording system of the ticketing agency, as regulated by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union.

## CHAPTER II

## ON THE TICKETING NOTIFICATION

**Art. 4** Except as provided in § 5 of art. 3, after verification of the regularity and consistency of the AIT, the ticketing agency will issue, within a maximum period of 30 (thirty) days counted from the date of the infraction, the NA addressed to the vehicle owner, which must contain the minimum data defined in art. 280 of the CTB.

**§ 1** Failure to issue the NA within the period provided in the *caput* will result in the filing of the AIT.

**§ 2** The NA will contain the date of the deadline for the presentation of the defense of the ticketing by the vehicle owner, principal driver or the duly identified offending driver, which shall not be less than 30 (thirty) days, counted from the date of issuance of the NA or publication by edict, observing the provisions of art. 14\.

**§ 3** The traffic authority may use technological means to verify the regularity and consistency of the AIT.

**§ 4** The data of the driver identified in the AIT must appear in the NA, observing specific regulations.

**§ 5** It is mandatory to immediately update the national database, by the executive traffic agencies and entities of the States and the Federal District, whenever there is a change in the vehicle and driver registration data.

**§ 6** For NAs issued before April 12, 2021, the period provided in § 2 shall not be less than 15 (fifteen) days.

### Section I

### Identification of the Offending Driver

**Art. 5** If the driver of the vehicle is responsible for the infraction, is not the owner or the principal driver of the vehicle and is not identified at the time of the infraction, the owner or principal driver of the vehicle must indicate the actual offending driver, by means of a form for identification of the offending driver, which will accompany the NA and must contain, at a minimum:

**I** \- identification of the ticketing agency;

**II** \- fields for filling in the identification of the offending driver: name and registration numbers of driving documents, identification and CPF;

**III** \- field for the vehicle owner's signature;

**IV** \- field for the offending driver's signature;

**V** \- vehicle license plate and AIT number;

**VI** \- date of the deadline for identification of the offending driver and filing of the defense of the ticketing;

**VII** \- clarification of the consequences of not identifying the offending driver, under the terms of §§ 7 and 8 of art. 257 of the CTB;

**VIII** \- clarification that the indication of the offending driver will only be accepted and produce legal effects if the offending driver identification form is correctly filled out, without erasures, with original signatures of the driver and the vehicle owner;

**IX** \- address for delivery of the offending driver identification form; and

**X** \- clarification on responsibility in the criminal, civil and administrative spheres, for the veracity of the information and documents provided.

**§ 1** In case of impossibility of collecting the offending driver's signature, in addition to filling in the information provided in the items of the *caput*, the following must be attached to the offending driver identification form:

**I** \- for vehicles registered in the name of an agency or entity of the direct or indirect public administration of the Union, the States, the Federal District or the Municipalities, an official letter from the legal representative of the agency or entity, identifying the offending driver, accompanied by a copy of a document proving the driving of the vehicle at the time of the infraction; or

**II** \- for vehicles registered in the name of other legal entities, a copy of a document containing a clause of responsibility for infractions committed by the driver and proving possession of the vehicle at the time of the infraction, which must contain, at a minimum:

**a)** vehicle identification;

**b)** owner identification;

**c)** driver identification;

**d)** infraction responsibility clause; and

**e)** period in which the vehicle was in the possession of the presented driver, and this information may appear in a separate document, provided it is duly signed by the driver.

**§ 2** In the case of identification of an offending driver in which the situation falls within the conduct provided for in the items of art. 162 of the CTB, without prejudice to the other administrative and criminal sanctions provided for in the CTB, the respective AITs will be issued:

**I** \- to the vehicle owner, for infraction of art. 163 of the CTB, except if the driver is the owner; and

**II** \- to the indicated driver, or to the owner who does not indicate him within the established period, for the infraction committed according to the conduct provided for in the items of art. 162 of the CTB.

**§ 3** In the event of the situation provided for in § 2, the deadline for issuing the NA mentioned in item II of the sole paragraph of art. 281 of the CTB shall be counted from:

**I** \- the date of filing of the offending driver identification form with the ticketing agency; or

**II** \- the final deadline for indication.

**§ 4** In the case of a foreign driver, in addition to complying with the other provisions of this article, copies of the documents provided for in specific legislation must be presented.

**§ 5** The offending driver identification form may be replaced by another document, provided it contains the minimum information required in this article.

**§ 6** Traffic agencies and entities must register driver indications in the National Registry of Licensed Drivers (RENACH), administered by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union, which will make available records of driver indications in order to enable monitoring and investigation of recidivism and irregularities in offending driver indications, articulating, for this purpose, with other Public Administration agencies.

**§ 7** Once an irregularity is verified in the indication of the offending driver, capable of constituting a criminal offense, the traffic authority must communicate the fact to the competent authority.

**§ 8** For the purposes of indication of the offending driver, the principal driver is equated to the vehicle owner.

### Section II

### Owner's Responsibility

**Art. 6** The vehicle owner will be considered responsible for the infraction committed, respecting the provisions of § 2 of art. 5, in the following situations:

**I** \- if the offending driver is not identified by the deadline set in the NA;

**II** \- if the identification is made in disagreement with the provisions of art. 5; or

**III** \- if there is no record of communication of sale at the time of the infraction.

**Art. 7** In the event of the hypothesis provided for in art. 6 and the vehicle owner being a legal entity, a fine will be imposed, under the terms of § 8 of art. 257 of the CTB, and the NP will be issued to the vehicle owner, under the terms of specific regulations.

**Art. 8** For the purposes of compliance with this Resolution, in the case of a vehicle subject to a pledge or financial lease contract, commodatum, rental or lease not linked to vehicle financing, the possessor, regularly constituted and duly registered with the executive traffic agency or entity of the State or the Federal District, under the terms of specific regulations, is equated to the vehicle owner.

**Sole paragraph.** The notifications provided for in this Resolution shall only be sent to the possessor provided for in the *caput* in the case of a contract with a term equal to or greater than 180 (one hundred and eighty) days.

### Section III

### Defense of the Ticketing

**Art. 9** Once the defense of the ticketing is filed, under the terms of § 2 of art. 4, it shall be for the competent authority to consider it, including on the merits.

**§ 1** If the defense of the ticketing is accepted, the AIT will be canceled, its registration will be filed and the traffic authority will communicate the fact to the vehicle owner.

**§ 2** If the prior defense is rejected or not presented within the established period, the penalty will be applied and a notification will be issued to the vehicle owner or the offender, within a maximum period of 180 (one hundred and eighty) days, counted from the date of the infraction, by postal mail or by any other technological means capable of ensuring knowledge of the imposition of the penalty.

**§ 3** In the case of timely presentation of the prior defense, the period provided for in § 2 shall be 360 (three hundred and sixty) days.

## CHAPTER III

## ON THE WRITTEN WARNING PENALTY

**Art. 10\.** In the case of infractions of a light or medium nature, the traffic authority must apply the written warning penalty, under the terms of art. 267 of the CTB, which must contain the minimum data defined in art. 280 of the CTB and in specific regulations.

**§ 1** The application of the written warning penalty must be recorded in the offender's record after the administrative instance for judging infractions and penalties has been closed.

**§ 2** For the purposes of compliance with the provisions of this article, the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union must make available a specific transaction for recording the written warning penalty in the RENACH and in the National Registry of Motor Vehicles (RENAVAM), as well as access to information contained in the records of drivers and vehicles for consultation by the agencies and entities of the National Traffic System (SNT).

**§ 3** The written warning penalty must be sent to the offender, at the address in his record or through an electronic notification system, if available.

**§ 4** The application of the written warning penalty will not imply the recording of points in the offender's record.

**§ 5** The notification returned due to outdated address of the offender with the executive traffic agency or entity responsible for his record will be considered valid for all purposes.

**§ 6** In the case of notification by electronic means, if available, the owner or ticketed driver will be considered notified 30 (thirty) days after the inclusion of the information in the electronic system.

**§ 7** To comply with the provisions of this article, the executive traffic agencies and entities of the States and the Federal District must record and update the infraction records and driver data administered by them in the information databases of the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union.

**§ 8** The fine penalty applied is null and void when the offender meets the requirements established in art. 267 of the CTB.

**Art. 11\.** For infractions committed before April 12, 2021, the written warning penalty may be imposed on infractions of a light or medium nature, subject to being punished with a fine, provided the offender is not a repeat offender, for the same infraction, in the last 12 (twelve) months, when, considering the offender's record, the authority considers this measure more educational.

**§ 1** Until the date of the deadline for the presentation of the defense of the ticketing, the vehicle owner, or the duly identified offending driver, may request from the traffic authority the application of the written warning penalty provided for in the *caput*.

**§ 2** There is no appeal to the Administrative Board of Infraction Appeals (JARI) against the decision of the authority that applies the written warning penalty requested based on § 1, except if this request is made concurrently with the presentation of the defense of the ticketing.

**§ 3** For the purposes of analyzing recidivism provided for in the *caput*, only the infraction for which the administrative instance for judging infractions and penalties has been closed shall be considered.

**§ 4** If the traffic authority does not consider the application of the written warning penalty as a more educational measure, it will apply the fine penalty.

**§ 5** For the purposes of compliance with the provisions of this article, the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union must make available a specific transaction for recording the written warning penalty in the RENACH and in the RENAVAM, as well as access to information contained in the records of drivers and vehicles for consultation by the agencies and entities of the SNT.

**§ 6** To comply with the provisions of § 1, the offender must present to the ticketing agency a document issued by the executive traffic agency or entity responsible for his record, showing the infractions committed, if any, referring to the last 12 (twelve) months prior to the date of the infraction, if this information is not available in the RENACH.

**§ 7** Until the measures provided for in § 5 are made available to ticketing agencies, the written warning penalty may be applied at the request of the interested party.

## CHAPTER IV

## ON THE FINE PENALTY NOTIFICATION

**Art. 12\.** The NP of a fine must contain:

**I** \- the minimum data defined in art. 280 of the CTB and in specific regulations;

**II** \- the communication of non-acceptance of the defense of the ticketing or the request for application of the written warning penalty;

**III** \- the amount of the fine and information regarding the discount provided for in art. 284 of the CTB;

**IV** \- the date of the deadline for filing an appeal, which will be the same date for payment of the fine, according to §§ 4 and 5 of art. 282 of the CTB;

**V** \- field for electronic authentication, regulated by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union; and

**VI** \- instructions for filing an appeal, under the terms of arts. 286 and 287 of the CTB.

**Sole paragraph.** The ticketing agency must use its own document for fine collection that contains the characteristics established by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union.

**Art. 13\.** Until the due date expressed in the NP of the fine or while the suspensive effect on the AIT remains, no restriction will apply, including for licensing and transfer purposes, in the files of the executive traffic agency or entity responsible for the vehicle registration.

## CHAPTER V

## NOTIFICATION BY EDICT

**Art. 14\.** After exhausting attempts to notify the offender or the vehicle owner by postal or personal means, the notifications provided for in this Resolution will be made by edict published in the official gazette, in accordance with the law, respecting the provisions of § 1 of art. 282 of the CTB and the statute of limitations periods provided for in Law No. 9.873, of November 23, 1999, which establishes a statute of limitations period for the exercise of punitive action.

**§ 1** The edicts mentioned in the *caput*, according to their nature, must contain, at a minimum, the following information:

**I** \- edict of NA:

**a)** header with identification of the ticketing agency and type of notification;

**b)** instructions and deadline for filing defense of the ticketing; and

**c)** list with vehicle license plate, AIT number, infraction date and infraction code with breakdown;

**II** \- edict of written warning NP:

**a)** header with identification of the ticketing agency and type of notification;

**b)** instructions and deadline for filing an appeal, observing the provisions of § 2 of art. 11; and

**c)** list with vehicle license plate, AIT number, infraction date, infraction code with breakdown and offender's driving document registration number;

**III** \- edict of fine NP:

**a)** header with identification of the ticketing agency and type of notification;

**b)** instructions and deadline for filing an appeal and payment; and

**c)** list with vehicle license plate, AIT number, infraction date, infraction code with breakdown and fine amount.

**§ 2** The ticketing agency may publish a summarized extract of the edict in the Official Gazette, which will contain the information contained in items "a" and "b" of items I, II or III of § 1, with the full publication of the edict, containing all the information described in § 1, on its electronic website being mandatory.

**§ 3** The publications mentioned in this article will be valid for all purposes, not exempting the ticketing agency from making the notification information available when requested.

**§ 4** Notifications sent electronically do not require publication by edict.

## CHAPTER VI

## ADMINISTRATIVE APPEALS

**Art. 15\.** Once the penalties provided for in this Resolution are applied, an appeal may be filed in the first instance in the form of articles 285, 286 and 287 of the CTB, which will be judged by the JARI that operates with the ticketing agency, respecting the provisions of § 2 of art. 11\.

**Art. 16\.** From the decisions of the JARI, an appeal may be filed in the second instance in the form of arts. 288 and 289 of the CTB.

**Art. 17\.** \[Article numbering appears to have been misnumbered in the source document\]

**Sole paragraph.** In the event of granting of the appeal mentioned in art. 15, the appellant must be informed whether the authority appeals the decision.

**Art. 18\.** Only after exhausting the appeals mentioned in arts. 15 and 16, the applied penalties may be registered in the RENACH.

## CHAPTER VII

## ON THE AMOUNT FOR PAYMENT OF THE FINE

**Art. 19\.** Only traffic tickets issued from November 1, 2016 onwards are subject to the provisions of § 4 of art. 284 of the CTB.

**Art. 20\.** For payment of the fine by the due date indicated in the NP, the amount equivalent to 80% (eighty percent) of the original fine amount will be charged, according to the *caput* of art. 284 of the CTB, according to the following formula:

**I** \- original value x 0.80 \= amount to be paid.

**Art. 21\.** When the offender opts to receive the NP through the electronic notification system and chooses not to submit prior defense or appeal, acknowledging the commission of the infraction, as provided in § 1 of art. 284 of the CTB, he may pay the fine in the amount equivalent to 60% (sixty percent) of its original value, at any stage of the process, until the fine's due date, according to the following formula:

**I** \- original value x 0.60 \= amount to be paid.

**Art. 22\.** For payment of the fine in the period between the day immediately after the due date and the last day of the month following the due date, the original fine amount will be charged plus interest related to the month of payment, at the rate of 1% (one percent), according to the following formula:

**I** \- original value x 1.01 \= adjusted amount to be paid.

**Art. 23\.** For payment of the fine after the month following the due date, the original fine amount will be charged, plus the monthly variation of the reference rate of the Special Settlement and Custody System (SELIC), defined by the sum of the monthly percentages, not capitalized, published for the period between the month following the due date and the month prior to payment, inclusive, and additionally the rate of 1% (one percent) relating to interest for the month of payment, regardless of the day of that month considered, as follows:

**I** \- formula: period \= include month following due date and exclude month of payment;

**II** \- value: original value x multiplying factor \= amount to be paid; and

**III** \- multiplying factor: 1.01 \+ (the monthly SELIC percentages of the period).

**§ 1** The calculation of the late payment surcharge and the updated amount due, based on the variation of the SELIC rate indicated in this article, will be maintained by the collecting agency, which will apply the accumulated monthly variation of the SELIC basic interest rate, from the sum of the correction indices in the period published by the Central Bank of Brazil (BACEN), whose obtained index and updated amount will be defined with two decimal places, disregarding the others without rounding, as a way to standardize the resulting amount.

**§ 2** The additional calculation of late payment interest, not capitalized, with a fixed rate of 1% (one percent), relating to the surcharge for the month of payment, in which the monthly variation of the SELIC rate will not be computed, will also be maintained by the collecting agency, complementing the final amount of the overdue debt, valid until the last business day of the considered month of payment.

**§ 3** The debtor user of the imposed fine will be guided by text in the NP about the validity of the document for payment purposes, whose deadline coincides with the indicated due date, after which the ticketing and/or collecting agency must be consulted to obtain the updated amount for payment.

**§ 4** If an appeal is filed within the legal deadline and is deemed unfounded, the incidence of late payment interest must be considered from the closure of the administrative instance.

**§ 5** Filing an appeal outside the legal deadline will result in the charging of late payment interest from the due date of the NP.

## CHAPTER VIII

## ON THE COLLECTION OF FINES AND TRANSFER OF AMOUNTS

**Art. 24\.** The executive traffic and executive road agencies and entities of the States, the Federal District and the Municipalities, members of the SNT, to collect traffic fines of their competence or of third parties, must use the proper traffic fine collection document established by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union, in order to guarantee the automatic transfer of amounts related to the FUNSET.

**§ 1** The collection of the percentage of 5% (five percent) of the amount collected with traffic fines to the FUNSET account is the responsibility of the collecting traffic agency.

**§ 2** Traffic fines payment will be made at the collecting bank network.

**§ 3** The receipt of fines by the collecting network will be made exclusively in cash and in full, and may be carried out in installments, by credit card, at the risk of institutions that are part of the Brazilian Payment System (SPB).

**Art. 25\.** The ticketing agencies of the Union, to collect traffic fines of their competence, must use the Collection Collection Guide (GRU) of the Collection type, observing Decree No. 4.950, of January 9, 2004, and Normative Instruction of the National Treasury Secretariat (STN) No. 2, of May 22, 2009, and its subsequent amendments.

**Sole paragraph.** The collection of the percentage of 5% (five percent) of the amount collected with traffic fines to the FUNSET account by the ticketing agencies of the Union shall take place in the form established by the STN, of the Ministry of Economy.

**Art. 26\.** The other agencies, collectors of traffic fines, of their competence or of third parties, and collectors of amounts to the FUNSET account must provide information to the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union by the 20th (twentieth) day of the month following the month of collection, in the form regulated by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union.

**Art. 27\.** The collecting agencies may enter into, at no cost to themselves, technical-operational agreements and partnerships to enable the payment of traffic fines and other debts related to the vehicle with debit or credit cards, providing offenders or vehicle owners with alternatives to settle their debts in cash or in monthly installments, with the immediate regularization of the vehicle's situation.

**§ 1** The collecting agencies must request authorization from the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union to enable the payment of traffic fines and other debts related to vehicles with debit or credit cards.

**§ 2** The authorization mentioned in § 1 will be issued by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union by means of an official letter to the maximum director of the requesting entity.

**§ 3** The collecting agencies authorized by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union may promote the accreditation, through contracting or accreditation, of acquiring companies (acquirers), sub-acquirers or facilitators to process the operations and the respective payments.

**§ 4** The companies referred to in § 3 must be previously accredited by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union, in the form of a regulation to be issued by that agency, and be authorized, by a supervisory institution accredited by the BACEN, to process payments, including in installments, using debit and credit cards normally accepted in the market, without restriction of brands, and present to the interested party the payment plans for outstanding debts, enabling the cardholder to know in advance the additional costs of each form of payment and decide on the option that best meets their needs.

**§ 5** The charges and eventual differences in amounts to be charged due to installment payments via credit card are the responsibility of the credit card holder who adheres to this payment method.

**§ 6** The collecting agencies that adopt this method of collecting fines through debit or credit cards must send monthly reports to the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union, containing the collected amount in a detailed manner, for control purposes of transfers related to the FUNSET.

**§ 7** In the absence of accountability referred to in § 6, the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union may suspend the authorization for collecting agencies to allow installment or cash payment of traffic fines through debit or credit cards.

**§ 8** The installment may include one or more traffic fines linked to the vehicle.

**§ 9** The approval and execution of the installment through the credit card by the credit card operator releases the vehicle licensing and the respective issuance of the Vehicle Registration and Licensing Certificate in digital media (CRLV-e).

**§ 10\.** The installment payment of already overdue fines must be added to late payment interest equivalent to the SELIC reference rate, under the terms of § 4 of art. 284 of the CTB, as regulated by arts. 22 and 23 of this Resolution.

**§ 11\.** The total amount of the installment, excluding the fee on the credit card operation, must be considered as collected revenue, for the purposes of resource application, according to art. 320 of the CTB, as well as for the generating fact of the transfer related to the FUNSET.

**§ 12\.** The following are excluded from the installment provided for in this article:

**I** \- fines registered as active debt;

**II** \- installment payments registered under administrative collection;

**III** \- vehicles licensed in other Federation Units; and

**IV** \- fines applied by other ticketing agencies that do not authorize installment or collection through credit or debit cards.

**§ 13\.** The ticketing agency is competent to authorize the installment, on a facultative basis, and may delegate such competence, in the form of art. 25 of the CTB.

**§ 14\.** The maximum executive traffic agency of the Union will be responsible for authorizing and supervising the operations of traffic agencies that adopt the installment modality with credit cards for the payment of traffic fines, as well as for accrediting companies, regulating the provisions of this article.

**§ 15\.** The accreditation of legal entities for the provision of the services provided for in this Resolution will be carried out exclusively by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union and must be preceded by proof of:

**I** \- legal qualification;

**II** \- fiscal and labor regularity;

**III** \- economic-financial qualification; and

**IV** \- technical qualification.

## CHAPTER IX

## FINAL PROVISIONS

**Art. 28\.** In the cases of vehicles registered in the name of diplomatic missions, career consular offices or representations of international organizations and their members, the notifications provided for in this Resolution, respecting the provisions of § 3 of art. 10, must be sent to the address in the vehicle registration with the executive traffic agency of the State or the Federal District and communicated to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for the appropriate measures, in the form defined by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union.

**Art. 29\.** The counting of deadlines for the presentation of drivers and filing of the defense of the ticketing and the appeals provided for in this Resolution shall be in consecutive days, excluding the day of notification or publication by edict, and including the due date.

**Sole paragraph.** The deadline is considered extended until the 1st (first) business day if the due date falls on a holiday, Saturday, Sunday, a day with no working hours or if working hours end before the normal time.

**Art. 30\.** The issuance of notifications provided for in this Resolution shall be characterized by:

**I** \- delivery of the notification by the ticketing agency to the company responsible for its sending, when postal shipment is used; or

**II** \- electronic sending of the notification by the ticketing agency of the vehicle, when an electronic notification system is used.

**Art. 31\.** In the event of failure in the notifications provided for in this Resolution, the traffic authority may redo the act, observing the statute of limitations periods.

**Art. 32\.** The NA and the NP must be sent to the natural or legal person that appears as the owner of the vehicle on the date of the infraction, respecting the provisions of § 3 of art. 10\.

**§ 1** If the AIT is not in the vehicle's record on the date of the ownership transfer registration, the current owner will be considered notified when the executive traffic agency or entity sends the extract for payment of the Motor Vehicle Property Tax (IPVA) and other debts linked to the vehicle, or when the annual licensing deadline expires.

**§ 2** The maximum executive traffic agency of the Union must take the necessary measures to provide the traffic agencies responsible for issuing notifications with the data of the natural or legal person that appeared as the owner of the vehicle on the date of the infraction.

**§ 3** Until the information provided for in § 2 is made available, notifications sent to the current owner will be considered valid for all purposes, and the current owner may inform the ticketing agency of the data of the previous owner for continuity of the notification process.

**§ 4** After selling the vehicle, if there is an AIT in his name, the natural or legal person that appears as the owner of the vehicle on the date of the infraction must arrange for the update of his address with the executive traffic agency or entity responsible for registering the vehicle.

**§ 5** If the address update provided for in § 4 is not made, the notification returned for this reason will be considered valid for all purposes.

**Art. 33\.** It is permitted to anticipate the payment of the amount corresponding to the fine, with the ticketing agency, at any stage of the administrative process, without prejudice to the continuity of the procedures provided for in this Resolution for issuing notifications, filing the defense of the ticketing and the respective appeals.

**Sole paragraph.** If payment has been made in advance, as provided for in the *caput*, the NP must be issued with the information that the fine has been paid, with the indication of the deadline for filing the appeal and without a barcode for payment.

**Art. 34\.** The procedures for filing the defense of the ticketing and appeals provided for in this Resolution will comply with the provisions of specific regulations.

**Art. 35\.** The provisions of this Resolution apply, as applicable, to ticketing cases in which the responsibility for the infractions is not of the vehicle owner or driver, until the procedures are defined by specific regulations.

**Art. 36\.** The statute of limitations periods provided for in Law No. 9.873, of 1999, apply to this Resolution.

**Sole paragraph.** The maximum executive traffic agency of the Union will define the procedures for uniform application of the precepts of the Law mentioned in the *caput* by the other agencies and entities of the SNT.

**Art. 37\.** The maximum executive traffic agency of the Union is authorized to issue complementary regulations for the faithful compliance with the provisions contained in this Resolution.

**Art. 38\.** The following CONTRAN Resolutions are revoked:

**I** \- No. 156, of April 22, 2004; **II** \- No. 424, of November 27, 2012; **III** \- No. 442, of June 25, 2013; **IV** \- No. 574, of December 16, 2015; **V** \- No. 619, of September 6, 2016; **VI** \- No. 697, of October 10, 2017; **VII** \- No. 736, of July 5, 2018; and **VIII** \- No. 845, of April 8, 2021\.

**Art. 39\.** CONTRAN Deliberation No. 115, of September 28, 2011, is revoked.

**Art. 40\.** This Resolution comes into force on April 1, 2022\.

### 4\. Didactic-pedagogical approach

The theoretical classes must be dynamic, taking into account the participants' prior knowledge and their cultural and learning differences. It is important to emphasize that in addition to information, the contents indicated in the curriculum must enable permanent discussions that favor the acquisition of values, postures and citizenship attitudes in traffic.

The riding practice class, taught and monitored by the instructor, must be carried out individually in the vehicle, according to the workload determined in item 3 (three) of this Annex.

Learning assessment is a permanent process that must be carried out during the course, through continuous observations during the classes and activities, considering the participation and productivity of each participant. However, at the end of modules I and II, carried out in person or at a distance, the institution or public or private entity or the driver training center responsible for the course must apply an assessment test, in electronic format, in the form established in specific CONTRAN regulations, or in written format, with 30 multiple choice questions, with 4 (four) alternatives, mandatorily using the question bank provided by the maximum executive traffic agency of the Union. In the application of tests, in any modality, the random process must be adopted in the distribution of alternatives, in order to prevent the occurrence of identical tests in the same class.

The practical assessment must be carried out at the end of Module III (Professional Riding Practice). It will be up to the instructor to prepare a checklist, according to the guidelines contained in the Professional Riding Practice Manual, in order to assess the conditions for safe riding of each of the participants.

### 5\. General Provisions

**5.1** The total workload of the course is 30 class hours, with 20 class hours allocated to Module I (Basic), 5 class hours to Module II (Specific) and 5 class hours to Module III (Professional Riding Practice).

**5.2** A class hour is considered the period equal to 50 (fifty) minutes.

**5.3** The daily in-person workload will be organized to meet the peculiarities and needs of the clientele, and may not exceed, in intensive regime, 10 class hours per day.

**5.4** The professional who wants to exercise the activities of motorcycle courier and motorcycle taxi driver at the same time must take a course with a total workload to receive certification in one activity and, subsequently, at any time, attend only 5 class hours of Module II (Specific) and 5 class hours of Module III (Professional Riding Practice) with respective assessments.

**5.5** The course will be taught by professionals qualified in traffic instructor courses and/or by professionals who have training (technical or higher) related to the disciplines.

**5.6** The participant who has 100% (one hundred percent) attendance and at least 70% (seventy percent) correct answers in the questions related to the theoretical content and 70% (seventy percent) in the practical assessment will be considered approved. In case of failure, the participant will have a maximum period of 30 (thirty) days to take a new assessment.

**5.7** Certificates will be issued by the authorized agencies, entities or institutions that teach the course.

**5.8** The maximum number of participants per class must be 30 (thirty) students.

**5.9** For the classes and assessment of Module III (Professional Riding Practice), the institution will provide vehicles equipped in accordance with current legislation.

**5.10** The professional motorcyclist will take a refresher course every 5 (five) years, according to the curriculum provided in Annex II of this Resolution.

**5.11** The refresher course must coincide with the expiration date for renewal of the CNH.

**5.11.1** In order to align deadlines and not impose costs on professional motorcyclists, courses taken before the effective date of this Resolution will have their validity extended until the deadline for the second physical and mental aptitude exams required for CNH renewal.

**5.12** Professional motorcyclists approved in the specialized course and who take the required refresher course will have the corresponding data registered in their record by the executive traffic agency or entity of the State or the Federal District, informing them in the "observations" field of the CNH.

**5.13** In an in-person course taught by the executive traffic agency or entity of the State or Federal District, or institution/entity accredited by it, with proven full attendance.

**5.14** In a course in the distance/partially in-person modality, with Module I (basic) and II (specific) being taken at a distance and Module III (practical) must be taken in person.
