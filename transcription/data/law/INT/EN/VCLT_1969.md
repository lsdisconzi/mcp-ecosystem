## Articles

### Art. 26 — Pacta sunt servanda

**Theme:** Good-faith performance of treaties  
**ELI ID:** `INT.VCLT.Art.26`  
**Tags:** norm_type: treaty_obligation · scope: state  

Every treaty in force is binding upon the parties to it and must be performed by them in good faith (pacta sunt servanda).

---

### Art. 27 — Internal law and observance of treaties

**Theme:** Internal law no excuse  
**ELI ID:** `INT.VCLT.Art.27`  
**Tags:** norm_type: treaty_obligation · scope: state  

A party may not invoke the provisions of its internal law as justification for its failure to perform a treaty.
