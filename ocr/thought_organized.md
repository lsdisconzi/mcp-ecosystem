# A message — for the record, and for her, one day

> Draft script, organized from the raw note in `help_me_organize_this_thought.md`.
> Kept in your voice. Six short beats so you can record it in passes, not all at once.
> Use whichever beats feel honest on the day. Skip what doesn't.

---

## 1. Why I'm recording this

I come back to writing — and to talking out loud — when something is too heavy
to carry in my head alone. This is one of those times. I'm recording this for
myself first. And, one day, for my daughter, so she knows what these days were
actually like for me. Not the version someone else told her. Mine.

## 2. What grief looks like when no one died

There are many levels of despair, and many levels of grief — including grief
for someone who is alive. The person is there. The connection is not. Not
because either of us stopped wanting it. Because other adults, who should know
better, have placed themselves in the middle of it.

There was a time when the thought of being a month away from my child felt
unbearable. I dreaded it. It's been almost two years now. Almost two years of
her life that I have not been part of in the way a parent is supposed to be.

## 3. What I wish were happening

You'd think that when a family separates, the adults around the child would do
everything they could to protect her sense of family — even a family that
doesn't live under one roof. That's the bare minimum. That's what love of a
child looks like in practice.

What has happened is the opposite. With force. With consistency. And the
person at the center of it — the only person who actually matters here — is
the one being given no voice, no choice, no room to want what she wants. She
may not say it out loud. She may not even know it's happening. But a child
that age gets shaped by the adults around her, and right now she is being
shaped away from me.

## 4. To anyone going through something similar

If you are in a version of this — swallow it where you can, work around it,
do not give up, and remember you are probably not in the worst version of it.
That is not a dismissal of your pain. It is a reminder to keep going. I tell
myself the same thing. I am not in the worst version of this either. But it
is not okay. It is not nice. And I will not pretend that it is.

## 5. What I am actually doing about it

I keep recording. On my computer. On my phone. I send messages almost every
day, knowing she probably will not see them today, or tomorrow, or for a long
time. I am not doing it to "get in touch." I am doing it because I want to
hear her voice. I want to know how school has been. Her friends. What she is
reading. What made her laugh this week.

For now, all I want is for her to know — whenever she finally hears any of
this — that I thought of her every single day. That I missed her every single
day. That even the smallest act of telling her so was something someone tried
to take away, and I refused to let them.

## 6. Why I am saying this out loud

We cannot be quiet about this. Quietly accepting it makes it normal, and it
is not normal. It is a monstrosity dressed up in paperwork and politeness.
Our children, and their children, deserve more than this. They deserve adults
who protect the bond, not adults who weaponize it.

I love you, filha. Every day. No matter how long this takes.

## Recording notes (for you, not for the video)

- **Order to film in:** 1, 5, 6 first (easiest emotionally), then 2, 3, 4.
- **If you choke up on beat 2 or 3:** keep the camera rolling. Do not cut.
  That footage is the truest part of the whole thing — use it or don't, but
  film it.
- **Length target:** ~3–4 minutes total if you read it straight; ~6–8 if you
  let yourself breathe between beats. Both are fine.
- **Backdrop / "shader" idea:** something slow, soft, and slightly sharp —
  e.g. a slow gradient (deep blue → warm amber), or gentle flowing noise
  (Perlin / fluid sim) at low contrast behind you. Avoid anything strobing
  or fast. If you want, I can write a small WebGL/GLSL shader you can run
  full-screen on a second monitor as your background. Just say the word and
  tell me: warm or cool palette, and abstract-flow vs. soft-particles.
- **One-line caption option** (if you post or archive it):
  > "For my daughter. So that one day she knows I never stopped showing up."
