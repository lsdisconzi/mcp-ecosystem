The main pages for jurisprudence documents search in Chile are as follows:

### 🏛️ Unified Jurisprudence Search (Buscador Unificado de Fallos)
The core of the system is the **Buscador Unificado de Fallos del Poder Judicial**, accessible via `juris.pjud.cl`. This unified platform is the result of a major modernization project initiated in 2022 and officially launched in July 2025. Its key features include:
*   **Scope**: It provides access to over **1.5 million sentences** from courts across the country, including more than **240,000 rulings from the Supreme Court**.
*   **Technology**: The platform uses **artificial intelligence (AI)** to deliver faster and more accurate search results.
*   **Functionality**: Users can perform advanced searches using filters such as **Rol/RIT (case number), date range, type of norm, and specific legal codes** (e.g., Civil Code, Penal Code, Labor Code). The system also allows for saving searches, setting up alerts, and making annotations on sentence texts.
*   **Editorial Oversight**: The content is managed by an Editorial Committee composed of ministers from each Supreme Court Chamber, ensuring that the included sentences have jurisprudential interest.

### 📂 Specialized Search Modules on `juris.pjud.cl`
Within the main unified search portal, several specialized tools are available:

**Líneas Jurisprudenciales (Jurisprudential Lines)**
This module allows users to identify the different **jurisprudential lines** established by the Chambers of the Supreme Court. It provides a selection of sentences associated with each of these lines. Users can navigate by guided browsing (themes with the number of associated sentences) or use a specific search box for the lines.

**Salud CS (Health Chamber of the Supreme Court)**
This is a specific search section dedicated to the **Third Chamber of the Supreme Court**, which handles health-related matters. It includes a "Compendio" (compendium) on health issues and provides advanced search options within this specific jurisdiction.

**Lista de Buscadores (List of Searchers)**
Although the specific page `https://juris.pjud.cl/busqueda/lista_buscadores` could not be accessed directly, this URL likely serves as a hub that lists all the different specialized search engines available within the Poder Judicial's ecosystem, directing users to the appropriate tools for their needs.

### ⚖️ Specialized Jurisprudence Databases

**Human Rights Sentences Search (Buscador de Sentencias de Derechos Humanos)**
Operated under the domain `busddhh.pjud.cl`, this is a dedicated search tool for **sentences related to human rights**. It offers advanced search options by data, legal texts, and document content. This platform is linked to the Poder Judicial's Human Rights Affairs Coordination and supports the dissemination of jurisprudence in this critical area.

**Constitutional Court Jurisprudence Search (Buscador de Jurisprudencia del Tribunal Constitucional)**
Accessible at `buscador.tcchile.cl`, this is the official search engine for the **Constitutional Court (TC)**. It provides systematic access to the court's sentences and resolutions. Key search filters include:
*   **Keyword or free-text search**
*   **Case number (Número de rol)**
*   **Date of sentence**
*   **Competency**
*   **Type of resolution**
*   **Ministers involved**
*   **Constitutional article or legal body**.

This tool is essential for identifying the current constitutional doctrine and understanding the court's reasoning.

### 📊 Statistical Jurisprudential Tool

**Statistical Jurisprudential Scale on Moral Damages (Baremo Jurisprudencial Estadístico)**
Located at `baremo.pjud.cl`, this is not a sentence repository but a **referential statistical tool**. It provides **statistical tables or scales of compensation amounts** awarded in Chilean judicial sentences for moral damages (daño moral). It covers two main areas:
*   **Moral damage due to death**
*   **Moral damage due to lesions derived from labor accidents and occupational diseases**.

This tool is explicitly described as **"merely referential and non-binding"** and is based on a collaboration agreement between the Supreme Court and the University of Concepción.

*   **Compiled jurisprudential lines** and thematic compendiums.
*   **Specialized collections** on human rights and constitutional matters.
*   **Statistical data** on compensation awards for moral damages.

These tools are designed to serve judges, legal practitioners, researchers, and the general public, promoting transparency and facilitating legal research.
