## Articles

### Art. 2.1.1 — ICAO Annex 17, Art. 2.1.1

**Theme:** Primary safety objective  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN17.C2.S1.P1.Art.2.1.1`  
**Tags:** norm_type: duty · scope: contractual  

Each Contracting State shall have as its primary objective the safety of passengers, crew, ground personnel and the general public in all matters related to safeguarding against acts of unlawful interference with civil aviation.

---

### Art. 3.1.1 — ICAO Annex 17, Art. 3.1.1

**Theme:** National civil aviation security programme  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN17.C3.S1.P1.Art.3.1.1`  
**Tags:** norm_type: procedural · scope: contractual  

Each Contracting State shall establish and implement a written national civil aviation security programme to safeguard civil aviation operations against acts of unlawful interference, through regulations, practices and procedures which take into account the safety, regularity and efficiency of flights.

---

### Art. 3.1.2 — ICAO Annex 17, Art. 3.1.2

**Theme:** Designation of appropriate authority  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN17.C3.S1.P2.Art.3.1.2`  
**Tags:** norm_type: procedural · scope: contractual  

Each Contracting State shall designate and specify to ICAO an appropriate authority within its administration to be responsible for the development, implementation and maintenance of the national civil aviation security programme.

---

### Art. 4.1.1 — ICAO Annex 17, Art. 4.1.1

**Theme:** Preventive measures against weapons and dangerous devices  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN17.C4.S1.P1.Art.4.1.1`  
**Tags:** norm_type: definition · scope: contractual  

Each Contracting State shall establish measures to prevent weapons, explosives or any other dangerous devices, articles or substances, which may be used to commit an act of unlawful interference, the carriage or bearing of which is not authorized, from being introduced, by any means whatsoever, on board an aircraft engaged in civil aviation.

---

### Art. 4.2.1 — ICAO Annex 17, Art. 4.2.1

**Theme:** Access control to airside areas  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN17.C4.S2.P1.Art.4.2.1`  
**Tags:** norm_type: duty · scope: contractual  

Each Contracting State shall ensure that the access to airside areas at airports serving civil aviation is controlled in order to prevent unauthorized entry.

---

### Art. 4.4.1 — ICAO Annex 17, Art. 4.4.1

**Theme:** Screening of passengers and cabin baggage  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN17.C4.S4.P1.Art.4.4.1`  
**Tags:** norm_type: duty · scope: contractual  

Each Contracting State shall establish measures to ensure that originating passengers of commercial air transport operations and their cabin baggage are screened prior to boarding an aircraft departing from a security restricted area.

---

### Art. 5.2.1 — ICAO Annex 17, Art. 5.2.1

**Theme:** Safety of passengers and crew during unlawful interference  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN17.C5.S2.P1.Art.5.2.1`  
**Tags:** norm_type: duty · scope: contractual  

Each Contracting State shall take appropriate measures for the safety of passengers and crew of an aircraft, which is subjected to an act of unlawful interference, while on the ground in the territory of the Contracting State, until their journey can be continued.
