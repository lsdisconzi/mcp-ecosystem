## Articles

### Art. 2.2.1 — Annex 18 - The Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air, Chapter 2 - Applicability, 2.2 Dangerous Goods Technical Instructions, 2.2.1

**Theme:** Annex 18  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN18.C2.S2.P1.Art.2.2.1`  
**Tags:** norm_type: power · scope: contractual  

Each Contracting State shall take the necessary measures to achieve compliance with the detailed provisions contained in the Technical Instructions. Each Contracting State shall also take the necessary measures to achieve compliance with any amendment to the Technical Instructions which may be published during the specified period of applicability of an edition of the Technical Instructions.

### Art. 10.1 — Annex 18 - The Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air, Chapter 10 - Training Programmes, 10.1 Establishment of training programmes

**Theme:** Annex 18  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN18.C10.S1.Art.10.1`  
**Tags:** norm_type: duty · scope: regulatory  

Initial and recurrent dangerous goods training programmes shall be established and maintained in accordance with the Technical Instructions.

---

### Art. 13 — Annex 18 - The Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air, Chapter 13 - Dangerous Goods Security Provisions

**Theme:** Annex 18  
**ELI ID:** `INT.AN18.C13.Art.13`  
**Tags:** norm_type: power · scope: contractual  

Each Contracting State shall establish dangerous goods security measures, applicable to shippers, operators and other individuals engaged in the transport of dangerous goods by air, to be taken to minimize theft or misuse of dangerous goods that may endanger persons, property or the environment. These measures should be commensurate with security provisions specified in other Annexes and the Technical Instructions.
