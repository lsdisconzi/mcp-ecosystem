## Detailed dispositions

### Art. 269 ter — KEEP (scope: PDI only)

**Verbatim text** (from `CodigoPenal_269bis_269ter.md`):

> "El funcionario policial, el fiscal del Ministerio Público, o el abogado asistente del fiscal, en su caso, que a sabiendas ocultare, alterare o destruyere cualquier antecedente, objeto o documento, o imagen o sonido contenido en sistemas de registro y almacenamiento audiovisual que permita establecer la existencia o inexistencia de un delito, la participación punible en él de alguna persona o su inocencia, o que pueda servir para la determinación de la pena, será castigado con presidio menor en cualquiera de sus grados e inhabilitación especial perpetua para el cargo."

**Scope decision.** The active-subject list is closed and exhaustive:
1. Funcionario policial (PDI included)
2. Fiscal del Ministerio Público
3. Abogado asistente del fiscal

A DGAC official is **not** within this list. Any DGAC-side omission or concealment must be pursued under a different provision (see Art. 228, Art. 193).

**Applicable actors on these facts:** PDI officer only (seg-263, seg-284, seg-285, seg-287).

**Verification prerequisites before reliance:**
- Identify a specific **knowing** act (a sabiendas) by the PDI officer — not the DGAC official — that concealed, altered or destroyed *antecedentes, objetos, imágenes*.
- Confirm the official *parte* and the EVID-001 CCTV record are documents within the article's scope.
- Establish subjective element (a sabiendas).

**Overlap note.** Arts. 193 and 269 ter may both apply to the same course of conduct (falsification/suppression of the official record). They are **alternative characterisations**, not cumulative charges.

### Art. 228 — ADD (correct prevaricación article)

**Verbatim text** (from `data/law/CL/CodigoPenal_Prevaricacion.md`):

> "El que, desempeñando un empleo público no perteneciente al orden judicial, dictare a sabiendas providencia o resolución manifiestamente injusta en negocio contencioso-administrativo o meramente administrativo, incurrirá en las penas de suspensión del empleo en su grado medio y multa de once a quince unidades tributarias mensuales."

**Rationale for addition.** The candidate review confirmed that Arts. 223 and 224 are limited to "Los miembros de los tribunales de justicia colegiados o unipersonales y los fiscales judiciales". Neither reaches DGAC or PDI administrative officials. Art. 228 is the **residual prevaricación** provision for every other public employee.

**Verification prerequisites:**
- Register `data/law/CL/CodigoPenal_Prevaricacion.md` as a bundle framework cache (currently not registered → status stays `not_in_bundle`).
- Determine whether any DGAC act amounts to a **providencia o resolución** in a **negocio contencioso-administrativo o meramente administrativo**.
- Confirm subjective element (*a sabiendas*).
- **Open point:** whether an oral administrative decision can satisfy the type without a written providencia.

All three are abuso-de-autoridad provisions applicable to a public official who fails specific statutory duties. They are plausible against the DGAC official but require:

- Confirming the DGAC official is an *empleado público* for purposes of those articles.
- Fetching the verbatim text — none is yet cached in the bundle.

### Art. 193 — KEEP (corrected: falsedad documental, not falso testimonio)

**Verbatim source:** `data/law/CL/CHIPENCOD_CP.md` (declared ELI ID `CL.CHIPENCOD.T4.C3.Art.193`).

**Correction.** The pre-review label — "Falso testimonio o acusación falsa por funcionario público" — is **not** the text of Art. 193. The actual article is a falsedad documental provision containing eight numbered modes. Modes 4 and 8 are the plausible candidates on these facts:

- **Mode 4** — faltando a la verdad en la narración de hechos sustanciales.
- **Mode 8** — ocultando en perjuicio del Estado o de un particular cualquier documento oficial.

**Applicable actors:** PDI officer or DGAC official (empleado público abusando de su oficio).

**Overlap note.** This candidate overlaps Art. 269 ter on the same alleged acts (alteration/suppression of the official parte or CCTV record). They are alternatives.

---

### Art. 194 — KEEP (corrected: falsedad por particular, boundary check)

**Correction.** The pre-review label — "Prevaricación por dictamen o informe falso" — is **wrong**. Art. 194 is falsedad in a public or authentic document committed by a **particular**.

**Scope decision.** Because the actor class is a particular, Art. 194 cannot reach the PDI or DGAC official. The pre-review preliminary view relied on the DGAC official's oral statements (seg-292, seg-333), which are not documents within the meaning of Art. 194 — oral statements do not engage the article.

**Retention rationale.** Retained **only** as a boundary check for the airline security official, if the airline produced a false public or authentic document. Absent that, the candidate does not arise on this record.

---

### Art. 195 — KEEP but RECOMMEND WITHDRAWAL

**Correction.** The pre-review label — "Prevaricación por omisión de denuncia de delito" — is **wrong**. Art. 195 punishes falsedad by the **encargado o empleado de una oficina telegráfica** who forges or falsifies *partes telegráficos*.

**Facts check.** No telegraph office, telegraph employee, or telegraphic despatch exists anywhere on these facts. The actors are an airline security official, airline crew, a PDI officer and a DGAC official. The documents are an official *parte* and an airport CCTV record.

**Decision.** Recommend **withdrawal**. Retained in the candidate list only so the record reflects what the legacy entry actually contained.

---

### Art. 196 — KEEP (corrected: use of false instrument)

**Correction.** The pre-review label — "Prevaricación por nombramiento ilegal o ejercicio de funciones sin título" — is **wrong**. Art. 196 punishes **malicious use** of a false instrumento or parte, treating the user as the author of the falsedad.

**Scope decision.** Derivative of Art. 193/194. Cannot be established before the underlying falsedad. If the same actor both created and used the false instrument, Art. 196 is absorbed by Art. 193 or 194 and adds nothing.

**Note on prior nexus.** The pre-review preliminary view cited the PDI officer's threatening language (seg-284, seg-287) as supporting Art. 196. That conduct is toward the passenger, not the use of a false instrument, and does **not** meet the elements of Art. 196 as written. Prior nexus rows on that basis should be re-examined or dropped.

---

### Art. 197 — KEEP (corrected: private-instrument falsedad)

**Correction.** The pre-review label — "Usurpación de funciones o atribuciones por funcionario público" — is **wrong**. Art. 197 punishes falsedad committed in a **private instrument** to the prejudice of a third party.

**Scope decision.** Unlike Arts. 193 and 194, the actor class is **not** restricted (*el que*), so the article can reach either an official or a private-party actor provided the instrument is private.

**Element requirements:** (i) private instrument; (ii) perjuicio de tercero; (iii) whether the instrument is a documento mercantil (aggravated penalty).

**Note on prior nexus.** The pre-review preliminary view relied on the DGAC official's oral statements (seg-333, "yo acredito en general"). Oral statements are not an *instrumento privado* and do not engage Art. 197.

## Methodological note for future reviews

This review changes a candidate's disposition from the pre-review state in **five** instances (193, 194, 195, 196, 197), not because the *articles* were wrong but because the *legacy labels* were. Label-driven review would have removed 193, 194, 196 (as I initially did in a parallel review) and kept 195, 197 under incorrect labels. Consulting the **verbatim text** is what makes the difference.
