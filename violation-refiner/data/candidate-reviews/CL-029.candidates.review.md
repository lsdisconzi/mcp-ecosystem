## Methodological finding

Several pre-review entries relied on **article names that did not match the
article's verbatim text**, and the pack's reliance did not always survive
scrutiny against the text it quoted:

1. `CL.LEY20285.Art.3` — the name read *"Toda persona tiene derecho a solicitar y
   recibir información…"*, which is the wording of the **derecho de acceso**, not
   the Principio de Transparencia. The verbatim excerpt was already correct. The
   name and the rationale are corrected here; **the excerpt is left byte-identical**.
2. `CL.LEY20285.Art.14` — the name read as a duty to *"proporcionar la
   información"*, while the excerpt is *"El Consejo estará integrado por cuatro
   consejeros…"*. The excerpt was correct; the article itself is off-point (see
   the demotion below).

Both are `article_name` defects **inside the bundle**, not defects in the cache:
the cache body for number 3 is unchanged and was already the right text.

A third class — **phantom enumeration** — produced `CL.LPDC.Art.3.h` and
`CL.LPDC.Art.3.i` by walking the alphabet rather than reading the article, which
ends at letra g). Both are withdrawn here. The same class produced
`CL.LEY19880.Art.4`, whose corpus file does not exist.

## The evidence-period question is unresolved, and this review cannot settle it

The `allegation_summary` describes conduct around an administrative complaint
referenced as *Expediente N°15126, opened 6 November*, but **every segment in the
pack is from 5 July 2024**. Nothing in the record supports the complaint-
processing sequence, the passivity of the CC'd supervisors, the three-officer
discontinuity claim, or the failure to address the PDI exoneration.

The election is a strategic decision for the legal team, and it is recorded as
`OQ-029-EVIDENCE-PERIOD-ELECTION` (`priority: critical`) rather than assumed here:

1. **Reframe CL-029 to the 5 July conduct only** — in which case the pack overlaps
   CL-008, CL-022 and CL-030 heavily and should probably be withdrawn or merged.
2. **Retain the November theory and expand the evidence base** — which needs new
   segments from the DGAC complaint file, a new round of open questions, and a
   revised `incident.date`.

`incident.date` is set to `2024-07-05` so that the structured field matches the
segments the pack actually carries. That is a factual change to the record and is
flagged for confirmation, not presented as settled.

## Methodological note for future reviews

Three defect classes recurred in this pack, and each is cheap to test for:

1. **Name/text mismatch.** Compare `article_name` to `verbatim_excerpt`; when they
   disagree, the excerpt is the evidence and the name is the claim.
2. **Phantom enumeration.** Never emit an article id by walking an alphabet. Read
   the article body. `get_article_body("<number>")` is the test, and it is the
   same call V03 and V04 make.
3. **Spliced excerpts.** `verbatim_excerpt` must be a **literal substring** of the
   body — V03 and V11 enforce it, and an ellipsis joining two non-adjacent letras
   is not one, even when every word inside it is verbatim.
