**Key findings:**
*   **Penal Code (CL.CHIPENCOD):** Arts. 194, 196, 148, 269bis, 269ter, 206, 256, and 255 are correctly identified and applicable. Art. 155 is about *allanamiento*, not abuse of authority (correct provision is Art. 255). Art. 143 is about unlawful apprehension, not violation of domicile. Art. 412 defines *calumnia*, not *injuria* (correct provision is Art. 416). Art. 150 concerns torture/appremios, not illegal detention (correct provision is Art. 148). Arts. 228 and 229 (prevaricación) require careful verification of their subjective scope.
*   **Civil Code (CL.CC):** Arts. 2314 and 2320 are correct.
*   **Consumer Law (CL.LPDC):** Arts. 3, 12, and 23 are correct.
*   **Aeronautical Code (CL.CACH):** Arts. 100 and 145 are incorrect for the described purpose. The correct provisions for carrier obligations and liability require verification.
*   **Constitution (CL.CPR):** Arts. 19.3, 19.7, and 19.4 are correct.
*   **State Liability (CL.L18575):** Art. 42 is correct.
*   **Data Protection (CL.LPDP):** Art. 2 is correct; the specific provisions for rectification and accuracy are Arts. 6 and 9.
*   **Withdrawn Candidate:** CL.CHIPENCOD.T8.C1.Art.390bis is a placeholder and has been withdrawn.
